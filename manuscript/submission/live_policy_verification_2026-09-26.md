## Elsevier generative-AI policy — verified 2026-09-26

Elsevier's current journal policy permits responsible AI assistance during manuscript preparation with human oversight and accountability. Substantive AI use in manuscript preparation requires a separate declaration. The policy's recommended declaration records the tool/service, purpose, author review/editing, and author responsibility.

The policy also states that:

- AI tools cannot be authors;
- AI use that is part of the research process should be described reproducibly in Methods;
- AI-assisted writing or editing of research code should be declared in detail in Methods when it forms part of the research process;
- explanatory images may use AI assistance with disclosure;
- AI-supported data visualizations must remain directly derived from underlying data through reproducible analytical/computational/statistical methods and relevant AI use belongs in Methods;
- primary research images may not be fabricated or altered by AI;
- general-purpose generative-AI image tools must not be used for graphical abstracts.

Official source checked:

- https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals

The current Structural submission figures are deterministic code-generated SVGs from frozen repository data/contracts; they are not generative-AI-created images.

The manuscript-preparation AI declaration remains required. Before submission, the authors must also explicitly adjudicate whether any AI assistance to research code falls under the Methods-disclosure requirement; this policy refresh does not infer that human decision from repository history.
