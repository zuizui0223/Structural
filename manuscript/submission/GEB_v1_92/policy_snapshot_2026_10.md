# GEB policy snapshot — rechecked October 2026, v1.92

This file records the submission requirements used to build the GEB package. It is a formatting/policy snapshot, not scientific evidence.

Official Wiley Author Guidelines currently indicate:

- Journal: *Global Ecology and Biogeography*.
- Review: double anonymous.
- Scope: broad-scale general patterns and processes in ecology and biogeography; macroecological, comparative, spatial and modelling approaches are in scope.
- Research Article: approximately 5,000 words in the main body is the journal's stated rough scale.
- Structured abstract: no more than 300 words.
- **Research Article abstract headings: Aim; Innovation; Main conclusions.**
- Title-page identifying information must be separated from the blinded manuscript.
- Analysis code/software and supporting reproducibility material should be made available under the journal's data/code policy; long-term publication requires a stable archival solution rather than relying only on a mutable development repository.
- Double-anonymous review requires data/code access to avoid compromising author anonymity.
- Wiley requires disclosure when generative-AI or AI-assisted technologies contributed substantively to manuscript preparation; authors remain responsible for all content.

The earlier v1.90 snapshot incorrectly carried the seven-heading structured-abstract format used by another biogeography journal. That formatting error affected no scientific analysis and is corrected in v1.92.

Re-check the live Author Guidelines immediately before the actual upload because publisher policies can change.
