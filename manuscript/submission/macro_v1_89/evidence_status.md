# Evidence-status statement v1.89

This note is intended for coauthors, editors and submission-package auditing. It is not a substitute for the Methods section.

## Empirical core

The paper's empirical core is the **global 5,401-island mammal analysis**:

- 1,275 pilot islands;
- 4,126 held-out islands;
- 79 pilot-selected focal species;
- 168 held-out spatial blocks;
- 325,954 held-out island × species cells;
- C−R3 = −0.001814;
- 95% block-bootstrap interval [−0.002817, −0.000997];
- 3.77% block-weighted relative log-loss reduction at natural prevalence.

This analysis is **nonconfirmatory exploratory** because it was run in a separately labelled lineage after an earlier one-shot protocol terminated on a physical-schema error. The manuscript must retain that label.

## Fresh evidence

Only the local boreal 19-island beetle system completed a valid fresh confirmatory primary. Its primary was **not supported**.

The global GIFT plant fresh attempt terminated after confirmatory response access began because a frozen checklist returned zero rows. It produced **no ecological primary score** and contributes zero fresh denominator units.

## Nonfresh / nonconfirmatory context

- The historical 318-island mammal system is a design-frozen nonfresh stress test. Its external-isolation contrast points in the same attenuation direction but did not support the original extreme-isolation amplification prediction.
- The endpoint-available GIFT continuation retains only 118 of 404 frozen confirmatory entities in 18 of 59 archipelagos. Its C−R3 direction is concordant with mammals but the subset is strongly selected and cannot support a cross-taxon replication claim.
- A-Islands and Tanzania are historical discovery/boundary systems rather than confirmation of the present source-network principle.

## Central claim boundary

Permitted:

> In a global nonconfirmatory mammal macroanalysis, the topology of the focal species' occupied insular source network retained natural-prevalence held-out information beyond a strong source-aware reference. The gain was broad but asymmetric, primarily improving true-absence probability assignment, and weakened with external isolation, species occupancy breadth and graph-source emptiness.

Not permitted:

- fresh global confirmation;
- two-taxon confirmation;
- improved true-presence detection;
- causal rescue or colonization;
- realized graph paths as observed dispersal routes;
- universal attenuation or a demonstrated unimodal response.
