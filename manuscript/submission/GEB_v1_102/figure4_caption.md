## Figure 4. A preregistered sealed rare-species layer does not replicate the exploratory source-network signal

**A**, block-weighted held-out C−R3 for the original 79-species exploratory layer and the prospectively sealed 96-species layer restricted to 5–12 pilot presences. Error bars show the frozen 95% block-bootstrap intervals. The exploratory layer supported a negative C−R3 effect, whereas the sealed layer's interval crossed zero. **B**, outcome-specific C−R3. The exploratory layer improved true-absence prediction but worsened realized-presence prediction; the sealed rare-species layer showed the opposite qualitative pattern, with strong improvement in presence log loss and no improvement in absence log loss. **C**, block-weighted C−R3 for the actual graph relative to the 20 preregistered degree- and edge-length-matched rewired graphs in the sealed layer. The actual graph was better than 11/20 nulls and its C−R3 was nearly identical to the null mean. The second layer is a preregistered species-wise validation within the same geographic system, not geographically independent replication.
