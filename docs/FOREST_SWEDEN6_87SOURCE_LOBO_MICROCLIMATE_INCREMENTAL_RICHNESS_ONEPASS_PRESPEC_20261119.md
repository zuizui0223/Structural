### ONE new exploratory ecological prediction question

> In **2021 recorded saproxylic beetle species richness per source-authored plot×horizontal shade**, does adding the **prior-year 2020 under-bark measured mean microclimate** improve prediction in a completely untrained Swedish study **region**, beyond strong climate, shade and spruce substrate reference predictors?

This is a different **out-of-region predictive transport** estimand from the 2024 authors' *within-source* analysis of shade, regional climate, richness and CTI. The source is **one country and six geographic Blocks**, not six global biomes. All original plots have Norway spruce log resources; a positive effect **would not** prove thermally suitable but hostwood-absent vertical habitat, temperature-dependent movement or causal thermal selection. The prior 2020→2021 lag prevents claiming simultaneous organism microclimate. **Known prior art**: original authors already reported regional climate × shade beetle associations. Prediction improvement alone is a narrow exploratory increment, not a new ecological mechanism.
