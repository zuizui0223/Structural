**Calculations:** Each island contributes **one** source-observed mean per response and taxon. For the interval, \\(\bar{x} \pm t_{6,0.975}\,s/\sqrt{7}\\), with \\(t_{6,0.975}=2.4469118511449692\\). The exact post hoc sign-flip tail is the number of complete sign assignments of the **seven original island-level effects** whose absolute mean is at least the observed absolute mean, divided by \\(2^7=128\\). The statistic and all 128 null transformations are reproducible from preexisting fixed v1.316 and v1.318 numerical island records without source taxonomic access. The method assumes a symmetric null for sign flips and independence for the conventional t interval; neither is guaranteed across the same archipelago, and no multiple-outcome adjustment was made. The p-values must **not** be presented as preregistered confirmatory evidence.

**Why this qualification matters:** Seven-of-seven leave-one-island-out means for Beetle spatial Simpson β remain negative (−0.2342 to −0.1111), as do 18/18 existing complementary original Event-half sensitivity estimates. But the seven-island 95% interval **includes zero**, and the exploratory two-sided island sign-flip p is 0.1875. **Directional stability to omission and resampling does not establish a population-wide negative effect.** The Beetle–Spider spatial contrast also includes zero, so there is **no supported formal guild × spatial-change interaction** at this geographic grain.

By comparison, the observed **within-site temporal taxonomic dissimilarity excess** is positive in every original island for both arthropod guilds, with exploratory seven-island intervals above zero and exact two-sided sign-flip tails of 0.015625. This does not eliminate incomplete-detection, taxonomic-revision, survey timing or environmental confounding.

**Source physical grain:** Seven islands; total source forest plots 29, of which 29 Spider and 28 Beetle had spatial beta values, and 29 Spider/26 Beetle plots qualified for half-sample sampling-noise comparison. The within-island 56 Spider / 49 Beetle plot pairs are **dependent**, as are 18 half-sample re-partitions and seven leave-one-island-out summaries. Original BALA v1.314/v1.316/v1.318 taxon scorers remain sealed; this audit accessed **zero** original Event or Occurrence rows. The paired guild-island comparison additionally has 29 versus 28 physically eligible plots, rather than a perfectly identical within-island pair population.
