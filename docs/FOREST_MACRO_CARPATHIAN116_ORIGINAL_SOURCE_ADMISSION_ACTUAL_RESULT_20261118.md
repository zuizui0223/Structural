# Structural forest macro — independent Carpathian 116-plot beetles × original forest environment admission result (2026-11-18)

## Decision: full original plot-ID match and environment/stand-block readiness PASS; guild-response prediction still HOLD

A separately published Slovak Carpathian primary-forest study (Gloor et al. 2026, *Journal of Applied Ecology*, DOI **10.1111/1365-2664.70594**) collected flight-intercept saproxylic beetles and measured canopy, lying deadwood, forest age, 1-km land cover and elevation across **116 physical plots** in **nine mountain ranges**. Beech forest 60 plots and spruce forest 56 plots belong to the one original national study; they are NOT 116 independent ecosystems, nine different countries or nine independent biomes. Authors have **already reported** deadwood/landscape associations with beetle species richness, red-list fractions and Sørensen/db-RDA community composition. Original public publisher data: Zenodo DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22142713](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22142713), CC BY 4.0, published 14-file manifest. New ecological novelty is **not** a repeated beetle–deadwood association. Any future claim must quantify a separately fixed **incremental out-of-stand or out-of-range predictive advantage** for specifically measured local wood resources beyond an adequate forest/climate/age/canopy/landscape reference, with a validated guild ontology.

The original publisher-file source-only and environment-only checks were **precommitted before original beetle species/abundance outcome rows were read**, on Draft [PR #469](https://github.com/zuizui0223/Structural/pull/469) branch `analysis/forest-macro-plot-environment`.
