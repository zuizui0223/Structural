Aggregate post hoc decomposition: delta T positive, delta U negative in both networks; no causal/global or normalized synchrony claim. ## Priority cohort: Wu, He & Spence (2020), EMEND Alberta
Original author DOI 10.1111/1365-2664.13687, original dataset DOI 10.5061/dryad.msbcc2fvq, published 2020-05-27; publisher lists EMEND_Carabids_15.csv (118.07 KB). Author article sections 2.1–2.3 confirm 4 preharvest cover types DDOM, DDOMU, MX, CDOM; 6 treatments R0 (=2% basal area retained, clear-cut), R10, R20, R50, R75, CT (=uncut); 3 complete replicate blocks per cover type, 72 compartments (~10ha), six traps/compartment sampled annually in 1999, 2000, 2004, 2009, 2014; catches standardized per 100 trap-days. Actual raw data support not yet verified. Author's preharvest 1998 sampling was limited and before final treatment boundaries. Do not call it a matched compartment baseline. Prior art already tested succession, turnover and retention-dependent recovery. Same EMEND network Lee et al. 2023 rove beetles (10.1111/ecog.06347) is not another independent landscape.

Primary contrast after source admission: 1999->2000 only, complete 72 compartments x 2 years = 144 nominal compartment-years, no missing=zero. Verify author source cover type/treatment/block/physical-compartment/year keys, exact source count/identity and explicit sampling opportunity, taxon column semantics and positive/zero/missing distinction; no label guessing. Use one fixed study-local incidence/Hellinger vector, all validated taxa, no selected species. Compute T and U separately within each cover-type x treatment group (3 compartments per group; only 3 unordered distinct-site pairs, not independent). Compare R0 minus CT within each cover type and average four equally weighted differences. Require both directional conditions for prospective descriptive match. Report four cover-specific contrasts; all intermediate retention levels descriptive secondary, never choose a better contrast after results. No significance, CI, dose-response causal claim or independent biome claim until original randomization/block dependence supports inference. Other survey intervals are not annual pairs and are excluded. This prospective protocol does not assume temporal or detection equivalence beyond verified source method. Full identity and effort gate required; do not use a partial balanced panel to rescue a STOP. Because forest cover types differ in species and trajectory, computing U across types would mix ecological strata.

Implemented and actually executed synthetic checks in analysis/forest_macro_global/emend_temporal_change_coherence_design_core.mjs (c0dde43107e02f255805e8a30935578332322b19). Constructed perfectly coherent groups with opposite change vectors: within-type U=1 in each, pooled U=-0.2. Thus a lower pooled U is not necessarily within-type desynchronization. Full 144-row synthetic fixture T=2, U=1 for treatment contrast, joint prediction fails as intended; incomplete source frame rejected. No original response accessed.
