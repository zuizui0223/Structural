## Prior knowledge has stronger coverage than previously stated

Wolfe et al. (2022), *Oecologia*, DOI [10.1007/s00442-022-05178-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00442-022-05178-9), already (a) measured **patch-level** generalist/specialist association on **734 patch observations** and found a negative binomial-GLM association (Fig. 5, nominal P=0.0000023); (b) reported a positive patch-size association for Stentor; (c) established different specialist and generalist response patterns, including specialist benefit of **matrix transfer**; (d) discussed why positive rescue and negative predator transfer might offset one another under **local corridors**. These are *published findings, not Structural firsts*. The published experimental unit for treatment contrasts is a 48-mL metacommunity microcosm; the 734 nested patches are **not** 734 independent randomized metacommunities.

The author-pinned GitHub path `Wolfe et al. /` contains only `README.md`, `Wolfe et al experimental data.csv` (176 landscape records), and code. Its `Predator coexistence.R` reads `final_count_patch_data.csv`; `Stentor patch size.R` reads `stentor_patch_size.csv`. Both named patch-level CSV files are absent from that inspected pinned Wolfe directory. Hence the published patch-level finding is source-supported *in the paper*, but cannot be independently cluster-aware re-estimated from this verified landscape CSV. The simple published patch-level `glm` does not include an explicit microcosm cluster term in the inspected script. This is an inferential caution, not proof that the paper's result is false; complete patch IDs and patch observations would be needed to check cluster-aware uncertainty.

## Why aggregate joint guild retention cannot identify the biological mechanism

The v1.212–v1.219 terminal endpoint is `J=I(G anywhere in metacommunity AND S anywhere in same metacommunity)`. It is **not** `I(there is a local patch with both G and S)`. A six-patch landscape can have the same terminal G and S totals and the same J=1 under:
- spatial segregation: G occupies patches 0,1; S occupies patches 4,5 (zero overlapping patches);
- local overlap: G occupies 0,1 and S occupies 0,1 (two overlapping patches).

Even if *marginal numbers of occupied patches* were known exactly as `g` and `s` of `n`, the sharp possible within-patch overlap is `[max(0,g+s-n),min(g,s)]`. For n=6, g=s=2, this is `[0,2]`. The 176-row CSV gives not even these patch occupancy counts. The known-truth fixture `scripts/wolfe_terminal_identifiability_v1_220.py` verifies tightness across all n<=6 and constructs indistinguishable maps. Similarly, a snapshot showing G in patches {0,1} at t+1 can reflect either no colonization (both present at t) or a new occupancy of 0 with source 1 already present. No t+1 landscape snapshot can distinguish these histories.

**This is an observation/identification boundary, not a novel mathematical ecology law or evidence of observed spatial refuges.** Wolfe's published discussion itself proposes size-associated refuges, but this does not prove them through the terminal landscape aggregates alone.

## Prospectively discriminating the ecological alternatives (future NEW independent data only)

The biological fork is **size sorting vs connectivity-driven rescue vs common prey/mortality shocks**. Size sorting predicts local differences in G/S retention across patch sizes and permits landscape-level joint retention even with zero imposed local corridor. True rescue needs temporal patch-specific 0→1 events tied to viable *prior* occupied sources and independently specified actual transfers; extinction/persistence (1→0/1→1) is separate. Shared-shock alternatives require effort, prey abundance, day effects, habitat and initial density baselines in the strong reference.

A defensible primary could ask: *Does an independently measured source- and guild-specific **directional transfer state** add predictive information for next-interval 0→1 colonization beyond pre-existing occupancy, local patch size, prey/abiotic state, nearest occupied source and assigned corridor/matrix regime?* Declare the estimand as heldout log-loss improvement on independent experimental metacommunities; do not pool 0→1 with 1→0, nor infer an operator from a coordinate-only kNN edge. Compare a true source-capability/transfer operator to a strong state/reference, not to distance alone. Precommit subgroup directions and multiplicity rules; no posthoc rescue if overall contrast fails.

Independent system intake must pass Structural v0.31 disjoint pilot/confirmatory partition and v0.32 + v0.42 estimability/response-quality gates **before** confirmatory response opening. Patch-level observational rows are nested, and should be held out by independent metacommunity *not* randomly by patches. Prefer a factorial experiment with patch-size heterogeneity **within** fixed patch number, and corridor transfer independently crossed with matrix mortality; a 'pure patch number' comparison at fixed 48 mL cannot also hold absolute patch volumes and relative size variability identical. Explicitly record this unavoidable intervention bundle rather than attributing any sign to patch count alone.
