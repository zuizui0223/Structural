### What this source can and cannot add

1. **Can** anchor a separately published, legally reusable observational comparison of forest-decline, salvage, deadwood/TreM and guild functional summaries in **three heterogeneous European contexts** with source-identified physical plots and original record provenance.
2. **Cannot** identify a causal vertical forest niche contrast: fir/spruce were mainly **1.5m flight-interception traps**, while oak was **~15m canopy and often volatile-baited traps**, with years, forest contexts and catchability varying together. Site-level `region/site/plot` uniqueness does not remove those detection confounders.
3. **Cannot independently replicate** the Finnish same-year **`Host_tree` three-guild species-incidence Hellinger² geographic-heldout `VD10` added-prediction** endpoint from these original 3 **98×82 derived plot-summary tables**: the original archive exposed aggregated guild catches/diversity/CWM/FDis/SES, not a verified 98×species-by-Host_tree incidence matrix or author-verified broadleaf/conifer/both trait annotations. `Abund_Xyl` and `Abund_Myc` are feeding guild summaries and not equivalent to Burner `Host_tree` affinity.
4. Original authors **already** used structural equation models for disturbance/salvage mediation via deadwood/TreMs and beetle guild diversity. A repeated `TreMs_div`→`FRic` or `Abund_Xyl` regression with this identical dataset is **PRIOR ART** and cannot be framed as a novel independent finding. Future distinct endpoint would need to be demonstrated in source fields with a prebiological fixed hypothesis and credible original plot/region holdout—not a post hoc positive-sign fishing expedition.
