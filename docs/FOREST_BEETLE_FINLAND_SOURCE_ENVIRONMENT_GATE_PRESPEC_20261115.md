**Original-author ecological measurements and meanings** (Burner et al. 2023 *Ecology and Evolution*, DOI 10.1002/ece3.10588; original source Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.8322080):
- `VD10`: local fallen AND standing deadwood volume (diameter >=10cm) estimated along transects, **m3 per ha**. This is an absolute deadwood resource amount, NOT broadleaf vs conifer species ratio and NOT an in situ 3D canopy index.
- `agedomin`: stand age based on five oldest trees, in years, NOT direct forest age of every plot tree.
- `OldFor_1km`: estimated *living* tree volume within old (>100-year) forests inside a 1km buffer, **m3**, a landscape old-forest continuity/resource proxy. It is NOT measured deadwood in that 1km, and not species-specific host wood availability.
- Paper measured climate, solar radiation and sample effort, so geographic, climatic and sampling confounds need an authored field mapping and appropriate spatial blocking before any biological associations. The original paper already used these variables for beetle dispersal trait-niche tests, so novelty is limited to a distinctly specified host-affiliation guild response and must not be claimed as new field observations.
