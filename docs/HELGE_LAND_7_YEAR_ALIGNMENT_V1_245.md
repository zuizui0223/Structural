# v1.245 — Do natal donor-year states align specifically with future observed immigration?

The original v1.244 output already showed a retrospective 0.04524 nat reduction in heldout next-year immigrant destination log loss when replacing historical-average natal-marked cohort sizes by year-specific counts. However, the result was better in only 4/7 birth years, with especially large differences in 2019 and 2020. These years were OBSERVED before v1.245 was designed; this is a scientific sensitivity check, not independent preregistration or a hypothesis-confirming randomization test.

Freeze exactly all 7!=5,040 one-to-one assignments of the seven observed birth-year mark-count vectors to the seven unchanged next-year documented immigrant destination distributions. Train only 1994–2013 historic source/destination routes and relative migrant detection yield. Keep farm/H, symmetric pair R2, and directional C2 with fixed tau10 (same as frozen v1.244), and recipient popularity P0. For each of four models, compute the matched alignment score and its percentile/rank among all 5,040 label reassignments. No subset choice, no conditional tuning, no inferential p-value: years have temporal structure, detection effort, secular trends and can be statistically dependent.

Also list all seven single-year omissions and all 21 two-year omissions of the ALIGNED score to expose the dependence on 2019/2020, regardless of favorable sign. Finally, for every one of the 11 source islands, ablate its current source-mark count state by REPLACING only its annual counts with its historical average, keeping other sources dynamic. This is a model-feature ablation, not removal of the biological source population.

Crosschecks are strict: v1.231 source hashes unchanged, v1.244 original mean score reproduced to machine precision, seven year sample counts 15,27,34,12,27,41,17, source marking effort never treated as true population size or a source failure, and all original mammal data and eBird closed. Source co-failure/recipient demographic rescue remain unmeasured and independent confirmation is absent.
