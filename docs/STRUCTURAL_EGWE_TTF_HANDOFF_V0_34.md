# Structural ↔ EGWE ↔ TTF handoff v0.34

## Core connection

The three programmes address **different levels of generalization**.

### Structural

> Does a spatial/structural coordinate retain endpoint-relevant information **within a system** after the declared current reference is supplied?

This is reference-conditioned adequacy.

### EGWE

> Does the declared present state contain enough information for **future fate**, or does origin/history still add a meaningful residual?

This is future-state sufficiency.

### TTF

> Does transition/turnover structure learned from some species predict structure in **entirely unseen species**?

This is out-of-species transferability.

The three therefore form a nested methodological sequence:

    support / admissibility
        → endpoint-transition estimability
        → within-system adequacy
        → operator portability
        → out-of-species transferability
        → residual origin/history interpretation

These are not interchangeable claims.

---

## The central distinction

A state or connectivity representation can be:

- real and useful within one species;
- portable across two biological operators;
- yet still fail to transfer across species.

Conversely, a cross-species predictor can transfer without establishing that the underlying coordinate is the correct within-system mechanism.

Therefore:

> **adequacy != portability != transferability**

This is the strongest direct connection between Structural/EGWE and TTF.

---

## Why the current genetic TTF result matters conceptually

The current fresh genetic TTF result is an empirical demonstration of the distinction above **in the genetic domain**.

Under its qualified fresh geometry:

- cross-species post-IBD transfer was not detected;
- the independently qualified within-species self test was significant relative to its frozen null.

TTF therefore resolved to lineage-conditioned spatial structure within the tested domain.

That result does **not** validate or falsify Structural connectivity.

Its methodological lesson is narrower and directly reusable:

> detectable within-system spatial structure does not imply a reusable cross-species field.

This is exactly the distinction a future Structural/EGWE → TTF handoff needs.

## Two future TTF lanes

### Lane 1 — state-transition field transfer

For each species `s`, first define a prospectively frozen transition state at location `x`:

    Z_s(x)

Examples could be colonization/extinction state, annual site-use transition, or another predeclared future-state coordinate.

Convert it to the within-species edge target required by TTF:

    U_se = psi_Z[ Z_s(i), Z_s(j) ]

where the edge transform `psi_Z` is frozen before evaluation-species outcomes are inspected.

TTF then asks:

> Does a transition field learned from training species predict `U_se` in completely unseen species?

This is the direct dynamic analogue of canonical TTF.

### Lane 2 — typed-connectivity predictor competition

Structural/EGWE supplies a response-independent predictor space.

For species `s`:

    R_s(x) = declared current reference/state
    C_s^p(x) = typed connectivity for operator p

Convert these node quantities to prospectively frozen edge features:

    R_se = psi_R[R_s(i), R_s(j)]
    C_se = psi_C[C_s^p(i), C_s^p(j)]

TTF then compares predictor spaces on the same species-disjoint split:

    T_R
    T_(R+C)

The main handoff increment is:

    Delta_C|R = T_(R+C) - T_R

Question:

> Does a typed connectivity representation add **out-of-species** transfer skill beyond the declared present-state/reference space?

A positive increment is a transfer result, not a causal mechanism proof.

---

## Species eligibility must not depend on a favorable Structural result

This is the most important anti-selection rule.

A tempting but invalid workflow would be:

    fit Structural within each species
        → keep only species where C-R is favorable
        → run TTF on those species

That conditions the TTF population on the outcome being tested.

Instead, species eligibility may depend only on prospectively frozen criteria such as:

- source/provenance validity;
- response-independent geometry support;
- measurement/admissibility support;
- predeclared endpoint-transition estimability rules;
- minimum sample/support requirements fixed before effect inspection.

A species whose Structural C−R result is null or adverse must remain in the TTF panel if it otherwise meets the frozen support rules.

---

## Burned pilots and TTF

Structural v0.30–v0.33 adds a prerequisite that TTF has only partly needed in static turnover settings:

> the future transition itself must be estimable before a confirmatory response is spent.

For a future dynamic multispecies TTF study:

1. freeze pilot and confirmatory time/sample partitions before pilot response access;
2. burn the pilot only to test event/class support under the planned heldout design;
3. exclude pilot outcomes permanently from TTF transfer scoring;
4. freeze the confirmatory Structural/EGWE state/connectivity representation;
5. freeze the TTF train/evaluation species split;
6. only then open the confirmatory transition outcomes.

Pilot feasibility contributes zero to the cross-species transfer denominator.

---

## TTF qualification remains an independent gate

Even if every species passes Structural/EGWE support and transition-estimability gates, TTF still requires its own deployment qualification.

TTF has already shown why:

- actual observational support can fail even after synthetic method qualification;
- heterogeneous geometry can preserve one transfer estimand while breaking another;
- within-species self-detectability can pass while cross-species Type-I qualification fails.

Therefore a future Structural→TTF route needs both:

    within-system qualification
        AND
    cross-species transfer qualification

Neither substitutes for the other.

## Interpretation matrix

### Within-system structure supported + transfer supported

Possible interpretation:

> The declared state/connectivity representation carries reusable transition information across unseen species in the tested domain.

Still not licensed:

- identical causal mechanism;
- universal spatial law;
- universal operator equivalence.

### Within-system structure supported/self-detectable + transfer not detected

Possible interpretation:

> Transition structure is system- or lineage-conditioned within the tested domain.

This is the Structural/EGWE analogue of the current genetic TTF Branch-B result.

### Within-system endpoint not evaluable

Required result:

> abstain.

A support or class-variation failure is not evidence of no connectivity effect and cannot become a TTF negative.

### Structural adequacy not earned but TTF predictor transfers

The TTF result can still be reported at its own predictive level if prospectively valid.

But it cannot retroactively rescue the within-system Structural claim.

---

## Relationship to origin/history

Origin/history remains a separate layer.

After a state/connectivity representation is frozen, one may ask:

1. does that representation predict future fate within systems?
2. does it transfer to unseen species?
3. after the transferable/current state is supplied, does origin/history still retain residual information?

A positive TTF result does not prove that island isolation, recent fragmentation, urbanization, or another origin has the same mechanism across species.

A null TTF result does not prove that geography/history is irrelevant within each lineage.

## Future combined hypotheses

A clean future multispecies dynamic programme could freeze four distinct hypotheses:

### H1 — within-system adequacy

    Delta_within(C|R) < 0

for the declared held-out loss.

### H2 — operator portability

If claimed, the same typed coordinate earns adequacy under a separately declared operator/endpoint.

### H3 — cross-species transfer

    T_transition > 0

under species-disjoint TTF inference.

### H4 — typed-connectivity transfer increment

    Delta_transfer(C|R)
      = T_(R+C) - T_R
      > 0

The four hypotheses are distinct and can disagree.

A strong paper would treat those disagreements as biological information rather than forcing one global positive/negative verdict.

---

## Claim ceiling

A successful full handoff could support a statement such as:

> A prospectively specified connectivity-state representation improves future-state prediction within species and adds out-of-species transfer skill beyond a declared present-state reference.

It would not by itself establish:

- one universal dispersal mechanism;
- identical histories among species;
- a universal fragmentation effect;
- causal identification of connectivity;
- universal transfer across taxa, scales, or endpoints.
