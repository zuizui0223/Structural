# Prospective contrast: direct donor loss versus source desynchronization

This document specifies a *new independent experimental system's* ecological inferential target; it is NOT a preregistration of any existing published Fox/Hopson/Helgeland responses.

## Ecology: two opposing responses to local population loss

Fox et al. 2017 Nature Ecology & Evolution, DOI 10.1038/s41559-017-0271-y, already demonstrated that forced local extinctions can enhance overall protist metapopulation persistence when dispersal has synchronized predator-prey cycles. This published spatial hydra mechanism must never be presented as Structural's original result.

A local loss has at least TWO consequences: **short-run source depletion** (fewer donor populations remain viable, reducing immediate recolonization opportunity) and **long-run correlation breaking** (surviving patches may no longer crash together, reducing all-donor cofailure risk). Neither "more donors is always better" nor "catastrophic loss protects metapopulations" can be universally claimed. The signed total effect depends on dispersal strength, original cycle synchronization and population/habitat quality.

## Proposed genuine independent perturbation

Use new replicated experimental microcosms in a taxon/system not already analyzed in Fox 2017 and not the Hopson or Wolfe datasets. Hold patch number, resource volume and geometry constant. Independently randomize (A) a viable-dispersal regime (low vs high, pre-calibrated *realized* movement), and (B) a nonpermanent **local population knockout** versus sham without destroying habitat. Randomization/experimental replicates must be at independently housed metapopulation units, not at internal time × patch rows. Do not treat induced local loss as field conservation advice. Environmental viability, prey resources, individual/patch detectability and census effort measured repeatedly.

A factorial design provides a direct testable contrast:
- Immediate endpoint: measured viable donor count and true target-patch 0->1 recolonization after perturbation, checked independently with replicate sampling.
- Delayed endpoint: time-varying source–source correlation (avoid nonstationary naive raw-correlation), all viable sources simultaneously unavailable, and true entire-metapopulation persistence.
- Primary total treatment interaction: high versus low dispersal modification of perturbation's effect on future persistence. Predeclare sign and time horizons **before new outcome access**; local donor depletion can be harmful even when long-run persistence benefits.
- Explicit competing explanations: direct donor loss without synchrony change; synchrony change without difference in conditional donor number; common prey shock; immigrant detection artifact; mortality displaced to recipients.

## Structural admissibility

Before using any new experiment as confirmatory, define source capacity as a time-lagged donor population and **independently measured directionality of dispersal**, plus an adequate lagged population/prey/environment/effort reference. Separate 0->1 colonization and 1->0 extinction. Fit models on disjoint independent metapopulation replicates, freeze an estimable pilot and independent confirmatory groups, and apply existing Structural v0.31–v0.42 gates before opening confirmatory outcomes. No favorable subpopulation/treatment selection after the result.

The original 2018 Dryad data DOI 10.5061/dryad.2rs23 is publisher-MD5-locked as metadata, but its original experimental README download returned 401/403 through all documented paths. The 2017 hydra effect is already published, so this source may eventually be a retrospective stress benchmark only. No source patch/replicate data have been opened.

## Conservation interpretation

Do not infer that actual biodiversity habitat destruction enhances conservation outcomes. Population knockouts in laboratory microcosms are not the same intervention as destroying source habitat on islands. A useful conservation hypothesis is instead **maintaining a diversity of asynchronously viable refuges**, even when the largest current exporter dominates annual migrant counts. The Helgeland posterior source concentration and v1.245 calendar alignment failure offer an empirical motivation, NOT a demonstrated rescue effect.
