## What changed

The project now distinguishes two ways a prospectively frozen held-out design can fail before effect estimation.

### Response-quality attrition

Too few held-out blocks retain enough usable target rows after the system's predeclared survey/completeness/effort rules.

This is the LandFrag-type failure.

### Endpoint/class collapse

Usable response survives, but the training data do not contain enough event/non-event support for the declared held-out fit.

This is the PNW/RMNP-type failure.

These are now separate gates. Neither can rescue the other.

## v0.42 future-only rule

Before a burned pilot opens, a new system must freeze both:

1. the normal v0.31 disjoint pilot/confirmatory protocol; and
2. a v0.42 response-quality attrition contract bound to the exact v0.31 fingerprint.

The v0.42 contract freezes the minimum number of held-out blocks that must retain enough applicable targets. Its minimum cannot be weaker than the v0.31 minimum estimable-block requirement.

The raw pilot remains the exact v0.39 three-column surface:

    partition_unit, block, target

System-specific response-quality failures are encoded as missing/non-estimable targets under rules frozen before pilot access. No new outcome-bearing column is introduced.
