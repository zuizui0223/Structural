# Prospective mechanism discrimination v0.1

## Question

The frozen A-Islands/Tanzania discovery sequence ends with one ecological hypothesis:

> generic isolation can decouple from focal-species source connectivity, and multi-hop source paths can carry held-out occurrence information that direct source distance does not.

That does **not** tell us why.

v0.1 freezes the next mechanistic fork for a genuinely independent system.

## Four explanations

### M1 — Contemporary colonization

Prediction:

**source decoupling increases 0→1 transitions.**

The response is not static occupancy. It is colonization of a previously unoccupied unit.

A source-decoupling effect found only among already occupied units cannot establish M1.

### M2 — Rescue / persistence

Prediction:

**source decoupling reduces 1→0 local extinction, or equivalently increases 1→1 persistence.**

This is separate from colonization.

Pooling 0→1 and 1→0 into one generic “transition” endpoint is forbidden for mechanism claims.

If both M1 and M2 are supported, the pattern is consistent with ongoing connectivity affecting both establishment and persistence.

### M3 — Historical colonization legacy

Prediction:

remote populations are genetically more closely related to the **frozen graph-connected source set** than expected from geographic distance, habitat similarity and sampling structure alone.

The useful discriminating case is:

- genetic source affinity is present;
- contemporary 0→1 / 1→0 effects are estimable but weak.

That pattern is consistent with historical legacy.

It still does not date a colonization event or prove that organisms followed one unique graph path.

### M4 — Environmental proxy

Prediction:

the topology/source-decoupling increment collapses after a **response-blind enriched environmental reference** is added.

The enriched reference must be frozen before outcome access. It cannot be expanded repeatedly until the topology effect disappears.

If environmental enrichment absorbs the signal and dynamic/genetic lanes provide no independent connectivity evidence, environmental proxy becomes the leading explanation.

## Why the dynamic split matters

A static remote presence can be produced by several histories:

- recent arrival;
- ancient arrival plus long persistence;
- repeated immigration/rescue;
- no current movement at all;
- omitted environmental suitability.

Therefore:

`presence ≠ colonization`

and

`presence ≠ rescue`.

The new framework forces colonization and extinction/persistence into separate estimands.

## Detection process

When repeated surveys exist and nondetection is plausible, the dynamic lane must include a detection process.

A 0 observed after a previous 1 cannot automatically be treated as extinction if the sampling design cannot distinguish biological absence from nondetection.

If the available data cannot make a transition lane estimable, that lane is **neutral**, not negative evidence.

## Required sequencing

For a future system:

1. the system arrives independently;
2. normal Structural v0.31–v0.40 admission applies;
3. the v0.3 extreme-isolation/source-decoupling hypothesis is frozen before response access;
4. every feasible mechanism lane is frozen at the same response-sealed stage;
5. transition-event minima are frozen before pilot response access;
6. burned pilot may determine estimability only;
7. confirmatory responses remain sealed until the normal admission chain authorizes a separate confirmatory protocol.

Mechanism analysis cannot rescue a failed Structural primary.

## Interpretation is not forced to one winner

Ecological mechanisms can coexist.

Possible outcomes include:

- colonization only;
- persistence only;
- both dynamic lanes;
- historical legacy without current dynamics;
- environmental proxy;
- mixed;
- unresolved because one or more lanes are non-estimable.

The framework deliberately avoids ranking mechanisms from the current discovery systems.
