# Biological source inversion vs geographic graph centrality — prior ecology

Smith et al. (2021; Ecological Applications; DOI 10.1002/eap.2416) established with 483 American marten genotypes, including 43 from Apostle Islands, that recolonization could arise after reintroductions within the mainland recovery network and then show gene-flow evidence going **from island to mainland**. The paper proposed protected complex island forest habitats and reduced carnivore competition as likely refugial drivers, but did not experimentally manipulate competition and did not directly observe telemetry paths for every inferred migrant. This is already a published finding, NOT Structural's discovery.

Another regional marten study (Journal of Mammalogy, DOI 10.1093/jmammal/gyab107) found signals of migrants between reintroduced populations despite limited admixture, emphasizing the difference between apparent movement and successful reproductive genetic incorporation. These separate lineages cannot be treated as fully independent repetitions of an island-source mechanism.

The ecological fork for Structural is directional and functional: a central geographic source node need not be a net demographic donor; an isolated refuge might actually be the most productive donor; and movement itself need not be successful reproduction. This distinguishes the predicted static kNN source pressure, temporal viable source output, directional movement, and subsequent recipient recruitment.

A robust new field test would require independently surveyed source and recipient occupancy at t and t+1, a predefined eligible island set including empty islands, direct or genetic movement links with sampling denominators, and recruitment/reproductive evidence. On matched Euclidean distance and source abundance, test whether net directional flow and successful reproduction track relative habitat quality better than graph node centrality. No such test has been performed by Structural in the current mammal data.

Why this matters for GEB: the existing 529-species IUCN-range-derived kNN signal is real within its mapped-label universe but does not establish any of these biological channels. Do not use freshwater Apostle genetic histories as an independent test of the original saline-island map. The Zhoushan 39-island field survey remains a metadata source candidate only: its official supplement returned HTML 200 and HTTP 404 at the two fixed PMC links, not a verified DOCX. No field incidence value opened.
