### Statistical definition and computational evidence

Each source sampling column is one species incidence assemblage (original beetle counts thresholded `>0`, not biomass). Among eligible columns, the trait-known species pool alone defines a 3-bin species-incidence host-guild proportion vector. Mean pairwise **sqrt-JSD** [0,1] is calculated over all original within-country column pairs; its **exact all-pairs mean** is accelerated by collapsing identical 3-bin vectors and multiplying by their combinatorial pair frequencies. Synthetic known truth verifies identical 3-vectors -> 0, mutually exclusive 3-vectors -> 1, species incidence Simpson beta formula, and trait-label shuffle leaving the original species-by-column detection matrix unchanged.

The frozen null independently shuffles only recorded **species' Host_tree labels within original country** (not original sample IDs, sites, frequencies, species detections or pair labels) `P=2999, seed=20261115`. All countries contribute one equal weight to the combined test, so Norway's 596,778 dependent sample pairs do not individually dominate the three-country statistic. The centered null 95% reference width is **not** a regional population bootstrap CI and does not resolve spatial autocorrelation or year dependence.

**Interpretation relative to this precisely defined null:** countries' captured saproxylic beetle assemblages exhibit **more divergent host-tree-affiliation composition than predicted if source species carried exchangeable host-affiliation labels**. In each of the three source-country matrices the within-country observed host-guild divergence exceeds its null mean, not only in Norway. This is evidence of structured **guild-sorting / functional composition differentiation** conditional on published species occurrence and the 3-category trait assignment. It does **not** establish causal local host-tree availability or tree species resource gradients; it could reflect biogeography, forest age, different capture protocols and times, trait-dependent occupancy, land-use geography or taxonomic coverage. `Host_tree` is a species affiliation, **not the measured host tree available to the trap**.

**No explicit conservation or ecosystem-service result:** No predation, resource consumption, woody biomass, canopy heat buffering, potential movement, management experiments or field forest-species covariance were measured in this test. It is not a spatial ecosystem-function β-diversity metric of equivalent units to the PREDICTS Simpson altered-reference difference +0.0942. Do **not** meta-average the two coefficients. It is a separate functionality-identity bridge across source regional samples.

## Additional source-method audit after endpoint: published physical-site hierarchy (2026-11-15)

After obtaining the original conditional-null outcome, independent inspection of the **author's original methods** supplies an important sampling-unit mapping absent from the Zenodo source CSV alone. This is an **after-result method audit**, not a second confirmatory signal and not a re-score:

- [Burner et al. 2022 original sampling Methods, *Biological Conservation* DOI 10.1016/j.biocon.2022.109491](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2022.109491) explicitly state **Norway 468 original physical sites**, mostly surveyed in repeated consecutive years, producing the same **1,111 sampling events** in the Zenodo species×sampling-column file (mean 2.4 survey years/site). The published authors **merged** repeated Norway events by source physical site for their local removal simulations. Our frozen Host_tree original score did **not** do that physical merging: it used 1,093 condition-eligible Norway event columns. Its 596,778 within-Norway event pairs **cannot** be counted as independent forest or independent physical site pairs.
- The same author Methods state **Finland 142 physical forest sites**, with **11** sampled twice several years apart, yielding **153** event columns. The authors treated the repeat-year samples as **separate local communities** for their original removal study because surveys were separated by years. For physical-site inference they remain *repeated sites*, so all 153 source columns are not 153 independent field sites.
- The source author Methods state **Germany 386 physical sites**, each sampled in one year, consistent with 386 source columns (376 retained in our conditional-null analysis). German original sampling sites additionally span several study networks and forest regions; their 386 sites are not 386 independently randomized experiments.
- More detailed field protocol is also in [Burner et al. 2021 *JBI* DOI 10.1111/jbi.14272](https://doi.org/10.1111/jbi.14272), which confirms Norway **468**, Finland **142**, Germany **386** original physical field sites and sampling across multiple projects **1993–2019**. The published **778 unique species across the three regions** is distinct from our 1,453 country×species rows.

**Revised strength of inference:** The positive **D=+0.091059 / conditional p=0.000333** remains an exactly reproduced estimate for the *frozen original sample-column population* and *species Host_tree label permutation*, not a physical-site or site-cluster-robust significance claim. The observed source-country sign is positive in all three data sets, but repeated Norwegian sites could alter the mean and null if merged by original physical ID. **Do not promote this to robust three-country physical-forest support before source-native physical-site crosswalk and a new clearly labeled post-result sensitivity test.** The original biological estimate and raw source data are unchanged. No justified map from individual Zenodo sample-column names to the authors' unique 468 Norway or 142 Finland site IDs has yet been verified here, so this report does not invent one.
