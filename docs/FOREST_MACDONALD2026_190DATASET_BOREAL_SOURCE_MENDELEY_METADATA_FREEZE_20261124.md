# Boreal saproxylic beetle nonrecovery: official 190-dataset Mendeley source admission

## Original independent evidence vs newly proposed macro effect

Macdonald et al. (2026), Nature Sustainability DOI 10.1038/s41893-026-01868-x (published June 29 2026), original meta-analysis obtained **190 field-derived datasets from 87 papers plus one unpublished work**, across boreal Europe/Russia and North America, and found saproxylic beetle community composition lacked a detectable recovery trend in the observed **16 conifer and 29 mixed forest years after clearcut**. This is EXISTING PRIOR ART, not a new Structural ecological result. An Aug 12 2026 author correction DOI 10.1038/s41893-026-01926-4 changed copyright attribution, NOT published biological estimates.

The authors explicitly anonymized individual primary study identities in publicly released similarity-level data because upstream original contributors had not permitted release of their individual data. Original Mendeley source DOI **10.17632/yt42chsskj.1** (version1; April 17 2026): https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/yt42chsskj/1, public title **Boreal Forest Biodiversity Recovery Curves**, with harvested-vs-reference and reference-vs-reference Chao–Jaccard similarities, plus Metadata_Entity_Level.xlsx. Public page lists Bugs_Arthropods_AllForest source files, which include multiple arthropod subgroups; do not infer that all are beetles/saproxylic beetles. The source is **not** a complete original species-by-forest matrix, and there is no plot-level hostwood/decay inventory or source-population census in the public source record.
