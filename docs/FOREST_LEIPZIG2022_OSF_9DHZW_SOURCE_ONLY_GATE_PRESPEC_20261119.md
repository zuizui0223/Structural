The England NFI 2023–25 national 83 source plot/year field network is preserved as a verified **method-confounded** Canopy(Lindgren) vs Malaise sample system: original 316/316 sequenced sample-header matching, 77 both-sequenced concurrent plot/year contexts, but a separately frozen within-NFI-region×year OTU null reached **`ORIGINAL_NFI2026_WITHIN_REGION_YEAR_EXCHANGEABILITY_SINGLETON_STOP`** before any original OTU rows. No future variant can be called the same frozen endpoint.

This new source intake asks whether a **single original German forest** supplies a distinctly useful **within-window-trap vertical × associated tree identity** dataset with verified sample units and open original records. It is a *methods/source* check, not a repeat of the previous failed England response and not an independent forest-biome biological replication.

Original peer reviewed source: Haack et al. (2022), *Response of Common and Rare Beetle Species to Tree Species and Vertical Stratification in a Floodplain Forest*, `Insects` 13(2),161, DOI [10.3390/insects13020161](https://doi.org/10.3390/insects13020161). The authors specify public underlying data in Open Science Framework, OSF [project `9dhzw`](https://osf.io/9dhzw/), DOI [10.17605/OSF.IO/9DHZW](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/9DHZW).

**Published prior art, not Structural's new biology**: one Leipzig floodplain forest with original species-level xylobiont beetles and original tree species `Quercus robur`, `Tilia cordata`, `Fraxinus excelsior`, 30 canopy traps at two reported canopy heights (20 and 25 m) plus eight understory traps, 13 roughly two-week samples in 2017, 205 selected xylobiont species from 442 identified beetle species. Authors already reported vertical and tree-species effects, rarity results, community composition and beta turnover. The 20/25m canopy window traps both used diethylene glycol, but understory had **ethanol**; it is therefore method/preservative-confounded to compare canopy vs understory even if windows are physically identical. **No original multi-height temperature logging, directly measured host-wood supply, accessible 3D movement or multiple independent forests is yet established.** Forest number = 1, NOT the number of traps/trees or replicates.

Another close precedent is Hagge et al. 2024 (DOI 10.1016/j.biocon.2024.110493), which already experimentally crossed 3 host-tree bundle species × 5 sun/vertical positions in 150 wood bundles; publisher [original paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0006320724000545) states raw data **available on request**, not an independently accessible publisher release. It cannot be substituted as open data, and its previously published tree+height effect is not a Structural discovery.
