# When does spatial topology become a biological mechanism? (v1.202)

## A biological fork, not another generic connectivity metric

The frozen mammal 529-species result is a **map-to-map** improvement, with a 90.18% mean-rewired-retained component and a 9.82% original-kNN-specific point-estimate component. Neither component shows animals dispersed along its coordinate-built edges. In addition, modeled source influences co-covered target islands instead of forming strongly irreplaceable source territories. The mechanism cannot be upgraded by re-mining that same mammal outcome.

The explicit ecological question is now whether **arrival at a previously empty habitat patch depends on a *directional transfer route from a source that was actually producing propagules in the preceding season***, after controlling for Euclidean geometry, habitat and detection. Unlike an arbitrary static network, this requires physically directional transport and time-varying viable sources. It also distinguishes colonization from protection against extinction.

## What the published experiments already answered

1. **California tidewater goby**: Martel et al. already used 190 estuarine sites, 813 eDNA samples in 2016–2017, and reported that occupied neighbours and their distance affect colonization. A repeat of that test is not a new mechanism ([Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.vdncjsxs9); [paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.15693)).
2. **California black/Virginia rails**: Van Schmidt & Beissinger already analysed rescue and the unreliability of interpreting isolation–extinction associations as rescue; the later 18-year rail study found most patch–turnover slopes stable under a period of severe nonequilibrium. These are **overlapping field systems**, not independent replicates ([Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.8w9ghx3h8); [2020 paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13460); [2022 paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.14111)).
3. A separate **Hall et al. 2018 black-rail** analysis compared spatially inferred dispersal with **23 genetic parent–offspring dyads**. Published mean-distance agreement is already a result; the accompanying Dryad lists occupancy and covariate CSVs, not an independently frozen, complete raw directed origin–destination event universe. It would be wrong to treat its result as new validation of the mammal kNN network ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.12811); [Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.3bb81dv)).
4. **Southern California giant kelp**: semiannual patch production data and ocean circulation-derived source–destination transport are public. Castorani et al. 2015 tested dispersal-related connectivity against demographic dynamics; Castorani et al. 2017 varied source fecundity and modeled ocean transport. Thus “connectivity matters” and “source production can change connectivity” are prior knowledge, not our discovery ([2015](https://doi.org/10.1890/15-0283.1); [2017](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2016.2086); [Castorani data catalog](https://castorani.evsc.virginia.edu/data/)).

## New *discriminating* comparison, not yet tested

The sharper missing distinction is between **static geographical graph information**, **directional physical transfer** and **temporal source competence**, assessed on truly future `t+1` local establishment and extinction. An explicit discordance test—sites equally near known sources but with very different *source-to-target* transport opportunity—would test whether geometry is standing in for movement opportunity. Only the combination of transport and active-source information can establish a mechanistic route. The opposite result would show that a static graph's apparent usefulness need not identify any such process.

Define colonization and local extinction as separate endpoints. Freeze the patch universe, temporal source state, target transition window, observation process and transport operator before biological values are opened. Compare local habitat and straight-line source distance, then coordinate-kNN graph, physical transport, lagged fecundity and their interaction. Prohibit time t+1 source states in t→t+1 predictions; never use target-year information to reconstruct an explanatory network.

The 1996–2006 kelp source pair is **already published and context exposed**, so analysis here could only be a **retrospective mechanism benchmark**, not a pristine confirmatory test. Moreover, the 2017 paper cites one transport DOI while the lab catalog presents a **version 3** DOI, and a separate 2023 ROMS-cell transport dataset. These cannot be substituted until exact file version, temporal universe and patch identifiers are independently verified.
