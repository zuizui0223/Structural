# Forest × beetle macroecology: when do resource habitats yield a community? (23 November 2026)

## Decision — one research mainline, no new biological response opened

**Candidate question:** Across independent forests/regions, is the beetle community assembled from the **joint availability of source-compatible hostwood/decay resources AND an independently accessible regional species pool**, rather than the forest amount, canopy height, total deadwood or resource composition alone?

This is a HYPOTHESIS/ROADMAP, NOT an experimentally confirmed global rule. Direct ecological mechanism remains unproven. This paper should aim to explain **when resource-based prediction succeeds/fails**, rather than simply repeating that global models are weak (Nyström et al., *Nature Ecology & Evolution*, published 2026-11-10, DOI 10.1038/s41559-026-03205-z) or that beetles differ among host trees/decay stages (ample prior art; Lettenmaier et al. 2025/26 DOI 10.1111/1365-2656.70183). Recently proposed SaproHabs taxonomy (Annals of Forest Science, published 2026-11-09, DOI 10.1186/s13595-026-01374-2) provides a possible shared vocabulary (22 functional forms, 34 types), **not measured retroactively by existing Structural field sources**.

## Three competing ecological hypotheses, with falsifiable out-of-region expectations

- **H_G: geometry/supply amount sufficient.** Forest amount, stand age, canopy structure, management and total deadwood explain out-of-region composition; taxonomic identity, decay-state diversity and independently measured source availability yield no additional held-region gain once effort is aligned.
- **H_R: resource *state* filters.** After identical observation and geometry reference, measured local substrate type, decay-stage/age, bark and microhabitat identities improve actual out-of-region species/guild detection prediction. This resource increment should persist where locally accessible regional pool is comparable.
- **H_RP: resource × source-pool gating (novel primary candidate).** A suitable local resource patch is insufficient when the corresponding beetle taxa have no independent accessible source populations. In matched resource-rich sites, divergence in the independently observed accessible source pool predicts species/guild absence/turnover; the resource-state × source availability term improves held-region prediction beyond geometry and resource state alone.
- **H_O: observation/ontology only.** After harmonized taxonomic resolution, matched traps/effort, authentic zero sampling and source study variation, apparent resource interactions disappear or cannot be identified. If source pooled labels are incompatible, report SOURCE UNIDENTIFIABLE, not biological null.

H_RP requires **independently measured source pools** (prior source-specific surveys with effort/years, or independently validated distribution/occurrence sources *earlier than target sampling*), not held-out target-site organisms, not a model-derived full-species occupancy map with target labels leaked, and not a near-me proxy or assumed absence from unstandardized GBIF-only records. Source forest connectivity and dispersal barriers must be defined from independently dated forest/resource map and source localities, not tuned after outcomes. Physical distance is a baseline, not proof of dispersal.

## 2026-11-24 MAINLINE PRIOR-ART RESET — restore functional CHANNELS, not merely increase current wood

The phenomenon of partial beetle recovery 12–21 yr after restoration is already published by Larsson Ekström et al. JAE2024 DOI 10.1111/1365-2664.14586 and Restoration Ecology2026 DOI 10.1111/rec.70592; these papers share the same Swedish 2021 field cohort/experimental site framework and may NOT be counted as independent replications. Restored stands have less total and poorer decay-state distribution than their target deciduous stands: unadjusted specialist deficit cannot be called an extinction/assembly debt **at matched current resource quality**. In an apparently contrary outcome, Roth et al. 2018/2019 DDI DOI 10.1111/ddi.12870 reported recovery of beetle species composition within a decade after LANDSCAPE-SCALE deadwood enrichment in German beech forest. Macdonald et al. Nature Sustainability 2026 DOI 10.1038/s41893-026-01868-x independently report no saproxylic beetle community-resilience trend in 16/29 observed postclearcut years in conifer/mixed forest.
