## Confirmed science versus unconfirmed inference

- Seven Azorean island contexts are **one archipelago**, not seven independent archipelago replications. Original 29 forest transects, 4620 source-frozen sampled Event opportunities, 10,379 published Event / 63,904 Occurrence source rows.
- In **seven out of seven original island means**, detected B1→B3 arthropod temporal Sørensen dissimilarity exceeds same-campaign disjoint half-sample heterogeneity (Spiders +0.1908, Beetles +0.2212). The exploratory source-island sign-flip tail was 0.015625 in each guild, without occupancy-detection correction.
- Beetle mean spatial Simpson B3−B1 **−0.1753** is an **observational sampled average**, with **4/7 original island means negative**. Island-only descriptive 95% t interval **[−0.4399,+0.0893]** includes zero and exploratory 128-sign-flip two-sided p = 0.1875. The paired beetle−spider contrast also includes zero. Do not claim a significant universal spatial homogenization or proven inter-guild difference.
- [Pozsgai et al. (2026), Scientific Reports](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-57146-5) already studied the same BALA data and reported both limited net aggregate change and significant **ground-dwelling richness decline**. Original turnover methods and spatial beta are a separate derived reanalysis, not independently sampled field replication. Resolve editorial novelty and original data author attribution before submission.
- There are **zero** new independent original confirmatory systems; original BALA biological scoring remains sealed, and GEB original mammal SCIENTIFIC HOLD is unchanged. No eBird records were used.
