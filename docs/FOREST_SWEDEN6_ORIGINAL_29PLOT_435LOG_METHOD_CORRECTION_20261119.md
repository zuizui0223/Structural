## Scientific decision BEFORE any original climate or species CSV row was opened

The original published methods of [Goßmann et al. 2024, *Diversity and Distributions*, DOI 10.1111/ddi.13836](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ddi.13836) establish **SIX geographical regions, 29 original physical field plots, 435 spruce logs, and three distinct source shade treatments**, rather than the 30 plots / 90 plot×shade cells presumed by a preliminary SLU README interpretation. The paper's figure caption states **five plots in each region except Siljansfors (four)**, hence (5×5+4=29) plots. Each original plot has **15 newly cut 1.5 m Norway spruce stem sections** placed along five transects: 5 sun exposed at the forest edge, 5 intermediate at 10m inside mature spruce forest, and 5 fully shaded at 50m. All 435 originally placed logs are nested in 29 plots and six regions: not 435 independent forests.

This is an independently published original-method source correction, **not** an outcome-dependent change. The previous (2026-11-19) author README intake precommit claiming 30/90 is retained as historical provenance and is **not silently rewritten**. Original species numerical records and original climate/CTI/richness rows have remained unread in all prior Structural SLU source diagnostics. ## Source-design and time meaning from original paper

- Original logger placement: **6 loggers per plot** (two for each shade level) directly under the spruce bark, hourly recording from **2020-05-01 to 2020-09-17**. No guarantee every experimental log had its own thermometer. Source original `Mikrotemp` plot×shade summaries are **cluster-level thermal proxies**, not log-day-specific 2021 conditions.
- The original shade treatment was **horizontal distance from forest edge**, *not* canopy/understory vertical height. Placement lengths: sun-exposed at edge, half shade 10m inward, full shade 50m inward. The logs were **cut at different positions along original trees and positions randomized**. Do not conflate original log source heights with vertical sampling height.
- Original beetles: experimental spruce logs exposed from spring 2020, emergence traps placed around spring 2021 and emptied autumn 2021. Original study already analyzed regional thermal gradient, shade×regional temperature interaction, species richness, total abundance, community composition and CTI; reproducing these is **prior art**.
- Original substrate measures: 2021 center `Diameter`, percent `Bark` remaining, and forest-edge `Aspect`, but these measures were covariates in the **published original paper**. A new Structural test must ask whether prior-year *measured* log-bark microclimate and substrate predict **geographically held-out** responses beyond original strong reference, and even that requires compatible source grain and a truly independent evaluation beyond published interpretation.
- Authors report `435 logs` but **do not infer full 435 beetle-trap detection opportunities** from positive species records alone. Missing occurrence rows are not verified absences.
