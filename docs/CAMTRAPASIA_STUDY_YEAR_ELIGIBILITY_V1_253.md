# v1.253 Study campaign year eligibility without capture values

v1.252 showed that seven already-confirmed non-domestic camera-positive mammal taxa had repeated Java/Sumatra detections from multiple source survey IDs but no matched two-island before/after IUCN2017 campaign evidence. Rather than retrospectively selecting species, v1.253 computes the temporally suitable **camera survey availability** for all 78 frozen study IDs across the four geographic natural-island candidates Bawean, Buton, Java, Sumatra, all three original heldout spatial blocks.

Only public survey metadata fields survey_id, year_start, year_end and effort are read; capture CSV is NEVER accessed. Campaign eras are fixed as strictly before 2017 (end<2017), spanning 2017, strictly after 2017 (start>2017), and unknown. Full survey effort is summarized within whole-study era categories only, not allocated to individual years. Missing study metadata remain missing; no reclassifications or changes to the already frozen 4-island geography filter. Four island identities are area-based GADM candidates, not exact original island polygon UUIDs.

The important distinction is temporal OPPORTUNITY versus photographic positive evidence: a study running after 2017 does not mean any of the original 529 shared species was detected there, and a study conducted before 2017 is not prospective external evidence of change relative to a 2017 compiled native mammal range map. Camera archive date and publication year cannot substitute for source study years. Neither GEB graph predictive skill nor biological rescue/extinction can be claimed; SCIENTIFIC HOLD persists.
