# v1.236: Existing pedigree dataset changes the feasibility assessment

The 2025 Saatoglu et al. *Journal of Animal Ecology* paper (DOI 10.1111/1365-2656.70049; source Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.qfttdz0sx) explicitly analyzed **recruit offspring and lifetime fitness of immigrant/resident parent house sparrows and their offspring**. Five source files, published 2025-03-25, are ARS_Survival.txt, LRS.txt, Rectype_ARS_Survival.txt, Rectype_LRS.txt, and README.md.

This changes the *data-access pathway*: our v1.233–v1.235 attempt to join disparate 2026 model and 2007–14 producer IDs properly STOPPED on irreconcilable natal hatch years. We must not revive that invalid ID join. A **different, internally consistent 2025 pedigree dataset** already supplies parental labels and recruits, directly aligned to the biological question. Source metadata are audited under the documented [Dryad REST API](https://github.com/datadryad/dryad-app/blob/main/documentation/apis/README.md). v1.236 only verifies source version and five exact files; no reproductive count, pedigree or outcome is downloaded.

**Crucial prior art:** The source paper already reports higher immigrant annual/lifetime reproductive contribution, benefits for hybrid recruits and increased exported recruits from immigrant parents. These are **not discoveries made by Structural**. A defensible *future retrospective* ecological extension would be destination/island-specific distribution and redundancy of recruit-producing natal donor islands, especially whether island size and number of immigrant arrivals fail to predict contributions to distinct destination populations after controlling for source variation. The file README's population/island code semantics and parent ID validation are required before such work.

No study is admitted as new independent confirmation: the 2025, 2021 and 2026 datasets concern the *same archipelago and overlapping subjects*. Original 529 mammal heldout, all v0.31/v0.42 future intake requirements, scientific GEB HOLD, and eBird-off policy unchanged.
