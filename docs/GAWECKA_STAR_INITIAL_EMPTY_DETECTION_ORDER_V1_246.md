# Gawecka 2025 v1.246 — When must a stepping-stone patch be **observed** before a distal patch?

The original *Ecology Letters* [Gawecka et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.70254) experiment consists of 5-patch STAR landscapes, 4 configurations of initial community placement (1C/1P/4C/4P), 3 food-web complexity regimes, 5 separately replicated landscapes per cell (60 independent landscapes). `kgawecka/recovery_foodweb_experiment` contains original `Data/experiment_data.csv`, author methodology, original output and metadata at public pinned commit `483ed8ae...`, with raw experiment file Git blob `18c1aef8...`. The authors already showed number and position of introduced communities and food-web complexity influence recovery (AUC); do NOT report these as a Structural discovery.

The *different* carefully limited endpoint here is OBSERVATION ORDER of patch detection in the one-PERIPHERAL-source treatment (`landscape=1P`). That condition fixes initial aphids in peripheral patch2, with four empty patches: center1 at one edge, peripheral patches3–5 at exactly two edges. Every path from source2 to any other peripheral necessarily crosses center1. Consequently, a distinct positive aphid-count sample in patch3/4/5 **prior to** a positive count sample in center1 would demonstrate that **recorded binary patch observations need not reveal the intermediary stepping-stone's use**. It would NOT prove impossible direct dispersal; aphids can pass through center transiently/at low undetected abundance between dates, and there may be observation artifacts.

The original author experiment sampled all patch counts approximately every 3–4 days. All first detections are interval-observed, not true individual arrival times. Freeze BEFORE biologic rows: all 15 independent `1P` landscapes, 5 per community type, 3 distal empty patches each; baseline verification source patch2 positive (introduced), other patches zero. Record for each independent landscape whether center was first-positive before any distal patch, on the same sampling date, after a distal patch, never positive despite a distal event, or whether only one side was detected. Keep all right-censored never-positive states, no missing value->zero imputation, no p-values/treatment selection, and report all 150 originally empty patch series from all 60 landscapes for context.

**Relevance to Structural:** Prior v1.222–v1.224 Hopson sample-zero models found observed absence and true extinction must not be conflated. A connected intermediate patch can transmit organisms without being observed above detection threshold at sampled times. Testing this with actual manipulated initial positions clarifies which occupancy-state observables are sufficient to infer a source→target process.

**No hydra result:** the Gawecka experiment introduced communities into initially empty habitat, not repeated catastrophic local extinctions of cyclic predator populations. Fox et al. (2017) already discovered "spatial hydra" persistence; Saade et al. (2022) already showed clustering of local extinctions slows recolonization. Our first detection timing is a different, descriptive, post-publication assay and provides no direct causal rescue claim or independent v0.31 confirmatory system. Original mammal heldout and eBird remain closed; GEB HOLD unchanged.
