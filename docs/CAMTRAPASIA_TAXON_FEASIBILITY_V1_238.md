# v1.238 taxonomic feasibility of real camera traps, ahead of geographic and biological rows

CamTrapAsia is a large independent camera-trapping compilation (Mendes et al. 2024, DOI 10.1002/ecy.4299; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.10780971) with 239 camera studies, 232 reported mammal species, 876,606 trap nights. The previously source-hashed site metadata and traits headers include survey latitude/longitude/effort and verified mammal binomials. The actual camera event rows remain unopened.

This study may contribute multiple surveyed large oceanic land masses (Indonesia/Malaysia), but **239 camera studies are not 239 islands**. Its scope includes mainland Southeast Asia, and study center coordinates may not identify island centroids.

Before reading any site or capture data, a precommitted v1.238 compares only the source `class=Mammalia` and `binomial_verified` columns from the exact SHA/MD5-verified Species_Traits CSV to the original frozen ultrarare 529 names. The name normalization is the PREEXISTING Structural v1.188 punctuation rule (period/underscore to spaces), not outcome-selected synonym substitution. It does not read any source species-by-site detections, traits beyond needed identity, original mammal pilot incidence or original heldout outcomes.

Any exact shared taxa permit ONLY a subsequent source-site **geography/effort metadata** examination and frozen island crosswalk. A global model skill result cannot be admitted without credible 4126-heldout island membership, independent survey deployments/negative detection denominators and native/historical status. The 10° global search index v1.235 is for regional pre-screening, not a substitute for biological evidence. No eBird, ALA, Hébert retry, BALA or GEB submission promotion.
