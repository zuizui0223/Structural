# Structural v1.258 — Island ecological claims and next independent-data gate

2026-10-19 | Source-verified scientific decision from frozen global mammal maps, three-wave BALA, response-opaque GSHHG, and real BioTIME pilot detections.

## Main biological question
**Does the set and arrangement of conspecific occupied islands change from a broad occupancy constraint to a possible presence opportunity across species occupancy states, and does that predictive change correspond to real colonization/rescue once donor productivity, detection and environment are controlled?**

Two distinct hypotheses should not be fused:

1. **Mapped-island conditional prediction:** In the original frozen 529 ultrarare mammal species, geography-derived kNN source-path features improved heldout predicted mapped-positive presence relative to an already source-aware Euclidean reference. The original kNN graph beat 20/20 coarse-rewired nulls. Yet **90.18%** of the improvement was retained after rewiring. The original network links are island-centroid constructions, not observed movements. This is a genuine constrained predictive result, not biological dispersal evidence.
2. **Biological source-loss/immigration:** Different donor correlations could alter which recipient loses access despite same source totals and pairs, but the four-donor construction is an *exact toy identifiability witness*. Of the 529 ultrarare species, **433 (81.9%)** have only 1–2 observed pilot donor islands, so the triple-cofailure model cannot by itself explain the majority of the pilot species. Neither the observed multivariate co-coverage nor the BALA prospective temporal non-support validates individual source-node demographic irreplaceability.

## Unexpected observation-derived contrast and why it matters

Published BioTIME 2.0 source-locked 19-study burned pilot has 123,584 species-site-year sampled detection comparisons. Among prior t-undetected recipient cells, t regional source-sites 1 versus 5+ have next-year sampled detection rates **4.65% versus 7.10%** (odds ratio **1.565**). Among prior t-detected cells, next-year sampled re-detection rates are **33.96% versus 73.74%** (odds ratio **5.459**). Thus the latter prevalence association is approximately **3.49 times** stronger on an odds-ratio comparison, with different conditional source-set definitions and a strong local sample-detection interpretation, NOT causal emigrant rescue.

The 1-versus-5+ continuation contrast is positive in **13/13 study IDs** where both denominators exist, but seven have >=30 prior-detected cells in each comparison tier and the full fourteen study IDs are not fourteen independent geographies. These are post-outcome descriptive comparisons only; no new biological rows were opened to make this note. Stable extensive source presence strongly co-occurs with stable *detection*; source abundance and effort cannot be discarded. This is the key empirical confound a source-network claim must beat.

## BioTIME cannot currently validate a universal literal-island mechanism

BioTIME metadata 708 studies -> physical screen 95 -> 19 burned study IDs + 76 reserved -> physical quality 47 -> 33 deduplicated reserved *network proxies*. Source-only GSHHG v2.3.7 selected only 2 terrestrial multi-island geometric candidates (373 and 375) out of 33. Published-method MD5-verified study audit showed both were Japanese Monitoring Sites 1000 studies: 373 infrared-camera large mammals (published coordinates deliberately coarsened to ~0.1 degree), 375 ground-active beetle pitfall traps. Their different taxa/sampling design and unverified archipelago membership make the pair unsuitable as independent same-species multi-archipelago replication.

**Therefore source gate scientific HOLD.** This is not a negative ecological effect estimate. Zero reserved 76 BioTIME taxon responses were opened or scored, zero new island source-movement/colonization mechanisms were confirmed.

## Defensible publication boundaries

- **Structural island occurrence paper:** Retain pre-frozen occupancy-state-specific predictive role inversion, accurately label original kNN as constructed geographic representation, and disclose that donor effects are not observed migration or conservation source population necessity. The A-Islands/Tanzania reference-conditioned manuscript has separate locked submission boundaries and must not be retrofitted with these analyses.
- **BioTIME community temporal paper, if pursued:** Focus on detection/re-detection and prevalence versus configuration using robust survey effort and data collection unit definitions. It is *global community macroecology*, not an island source-rescue study, and the 76-study holdout remains species-response sealed until a valid new protocol has passed.
- **New island dynamic ecology hypothesis:** For conservation mechanism evidence, require at least two independent archipelagos with source/site IDs, time-ordered occupancy, repeat-survey detection denominators and actual directional movement or seed/recruitment where possible. A source metric calibrated to future sampled detections alone cannot establish true colonization or rescue.

## Scientific stop/go decision

**STOP** further same-529-mammal threshold graph refinements, reused A-Islands confirmatory outcomes, exhausted BALA time-window rescues, Helgeland/Hopson/Gawecka post-outcome source-selection, eBird and BioTIME 373/375 as if independent island source evidence. No reserved heldout species values should be opened to rescue this conclusion.

**GO** only for a genuinely new, source-verified island/archipelago repeat-survey event universe that can survive site/archipelago identity and exact taxon detection eligibility checks before outcome access. Candidate datasets discovered in current survey include repeated A-Islands flora surveys (already used in Structural, thus not a fresh independent denominator) and a standardized Reef Life Survey reef-fish event program (marine-only, original 2014 archival data retain latest surveys rather than future time series; current live event-denominator provenance unverified). Neither is admitted without an exact source and fresh evidence gate.
