## Novelty and evidence limitations

Published original 2024 paper ALREADY tested region/climate × shade effects and CTI/community changes, so those associations are **prior art**. The new frozen predictive endpoint (E0 macro/ambient+shade/aspect; E1 add prior-year measured under-bark `Mikrotemp`; E2 add `Bark` and `Diameter`) asks whether substrate characteristics contribute incremental **held-region predictive information** to source CTI beyond microclimate, not a direct mechanism of host absence. Even an E2 predictive benefit would be exploratorily sourced from **6 Swedish regions in one source study**, not worldwide resource-locked three-dimensional forest ecology, population abundance, true vertical movements, experimentally independent 6 biomes or a climate warming causal effect. No specieslist log-level absence can be inferred: original species file is a recorded positive-occurrence list, not the complete 435-log exposure/zero frame.
