## Biological hypothesis that remains untested

A source-rich landscape can produce many opportunities for recolonization yet few **independently functioning** donors if the same dispersal process synchronizes their failures. The testable causal fork is:
1. **Supply:** more viable occupied donors and directional movement can increase next-interval 0→1 colonization.
2. **Common donor failure:** correlated collapses reduce the probability of any viable donor, even when static topology shows multiple reachable sources.
3. **Environmental proxy:** resource/prey disturbances drive both donor failure and target outcomes without source-driven rescue.

Rescue means a measured transition and defensible source process. Do not infer it from end-of-study joint presence or geometric nearest neighbours. Pairwise synchrony alone cannot identify all-donors-off probability (the v1.203 known-truth counterexample already demonstrated this); the remaining empirical novelty would be **additional independent heldout prediction** of actual 0→1 colonization beyond a sufficiently rich lagged reference.

## Published source-methods design and independence audit

**Hopson & Fox (2019)** (*Journal of Animal Ecology*, https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.12905, source https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.n8d6s43): fourteen distinct 15-patch metapopulations, seven randomized per short versus occasional-long distance treatment, two temporal blocks. Equal total dispersal (~10% three times per week), different length kernel. Crucial original Methods qualification: the **specific day-wise destinations of long-distance dispersal were shared by all replicate long-distance metapopulations on that day**. Fourteen independent experimental metapopulation replicates do not imply 210 independent experimental treatments, nor seven independently sampled daily stochastic migration maps. Authors already showed increased prey synchrony under long-distance dispersal. Measured individual movement/viable donor-to-target flux and absence-corrected 0→1 colonization are not verified from source metadata. **HOLD**, not newly admitted.

**Green et al. (Dryad 2022 / Journal of Animal Ecology 2023)**, https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.3j9kd51kx, publication https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.13943: 22 experiments, 16 distinct network structures, and four replicate experimental metacommunities per experiment; **88 potential** experimental units, pending exact IDs/overlap confirmation. It pools data including **Holyoak (1996, 2000)** as well as Anderson lab experiments, so it is not 22 new, source-independent cohorts. Independent network architecture types number at most 16, **not** 88. Tubes physically connect patches; the source summary does **not** verify measured per-interval directed animal migration. Authors already related connectivity/network synchrony to predator/prey occupancy. **HOLD**, not newly admitted.

Neither research system can be assigned a universal minimum (LandFrag's 30 independent geographies is NOT a universal gate). For future real intake, set an *honestly estimable* number of independent validation blocks, class/event counts and survey-quality survival minima **before** opening any pilot. Source identity/fingerprints, graph adjacency, temporal patch IDs, independent actual dispersal maps and prior publication overlap must first be resolved as allowed under existing v0.10/v0.11. Published biological outcome contexts mean a retrospective exploratory use cannot automatically become fresh evidence.

## Concrete future primary and decisive negatives

- **Primary:** among patch-species pairs at `z(t)=0`, compare independent donor capacity × time-lagged directional transport against strong reference `R3` (lagged patch occupancy and prey density, habitat/patch area, observation effort, time, archipelago/network baseline and nearest occupied-source distance), scoring genuine future 0→1 events under *whole independent metapopulation* heldouts.
- **If no 0→1 variation or no independently specified operator:** estimability STOP, not null effect.
- **If only synchrony correlates with future losses:** do NOT call rescue. Test 1→0/extinction separately, do not pool it with 0→1.
- **If C−R3 is null/positive:** no rescued claim via posthoc treatments, source richness, patch-count classes or synchrony subsets.
- **If operator effect varies by shared day-wise route realizations:** treat the shared realization as a dependence cluster, not repeated evidence.

**Current action:** no source biological rows reopened and no confirmatory eligible system added; all pre-existing failures untouched. The next valuable evidence is a properly identity-locked, response-blind new study meeting the exact v0.31/v0.42 design, not an unlimited search or version-count increase.
