# Sbaraglia et al. (2025) Central European deadwood beetles: 10-site randomized-substrate experiment, source eligibility ONLY (2026-11-17)

## Fixed hypothesis, prior art, and exact sampling hierarchy

Source [Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.zcrjdfnpj](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.zcrjdfnpj), published 3 February 2025, original publication [Sbaraglia et al. 2025, *Ecological Entomology* DOI 10.1111/een.13428](https://doi.org/10.1111/een.13428).

Original authors **already established**: higher captured numbers/richness in shaded vs sunny sites (not necessarily larger standardized diversity), site×season-dependent change, significant shade-related species composition, and limited/no fungal pretreatment effect; these are PRIOR ART, never claim Structural first discovery. Saproxylic animals arriving to deadwood on **adhesive trap** do not directly verify breeding success/host specialization or prey/predator/feeding guild. The source publicly describes 198 beetle species in a wide abundance matrix, **NOT 198 guild-annotated species**.

Original published field design: **10 geographical source localities** in Germany and Czech Republic. In EACH locality a forest-interior shady plot and forest-margin sunny plot (20 plots), EACH with 20 randomized wood bundles (4 tree hosts: oak, beech, pine, spruce × 5 fungus/sterilization/burning/control treatments). **400 physical bundles × 2 temporal collecting runs (May/June 2022) = 800 capture units**, 3 logs/bundle and one 15×10 cm sticky plate each; `time_exp` original exposure DAYS. Source matrix `stickies_final.csv`: README advertises **800 units × 198 detected beetle species**. Source `env_final.rds`: original factors `code`, `tree_species`, `treatment`, `locality`, `exposure`, `altitude`, `tree`, `run`, `time_exp`, `nspec`, `abu`. **Never count 800 as 800 independent forests**: source parent geographical unit is at most 10; sunlight is *paired observational site placement* within locality, while wood tree-species/treatment assignment is experimentally designed.

Rather than redo the original paper's greater shaded abundance/GLMM or its fungal non-effect, ask whether **tree-resource identity and sun exposure jointly support species community prediction at an entirely unseen parent locality, controlling for season and recorded effort**, and whether adding host identity improves out-of-locality predictive accuracy relative to source site-independent baseline. Any such effect is **within the same original ten-site experiment** and NOT independent regional replication or guaranteed mechanistic proof. This first stage estimates **zero new ecological coefficients**.
