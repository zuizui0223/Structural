# Piccini 2026: ONE floor-stratum detected-species feeding-guild transfer test, frozen before ORIGINAL biological response (2026-11-17)

## Purpose and prior art

The paper Piccini et al. (2026), *Journal of Applied Ecology*, DOI 10.1111/1365-2664.70472, has **already reported** stand/tree composition and within-layer feeding-guild responses. This is NOT a first new finding of such forest sorting. Its official source GRO.data DOI 10.25625/H8EMJS was independently qualified in Structural source-only audits.

The completely original source-only and unscored audits verify 8 independent parent sites, 5 stand categories/site and 40 named plot labels; 3 vertical stratum labels. Exact author source 'trapID' values are plot×stratum unique and observed in counts 8/3/3 in 37 plots, 8/3/2 in 3 plots, consistent with nominal 8 floor pitfall plus 3 and 3 flight intercept traps. 40 of 40 plot labels have an 8-trap-ID stratum. The three 'trap', 'trap_ID', 'trapID' fields differ in hierarchy. Missing 3 ID labels in a different 3-ID stratum have **unknown explanation**, not proven zero-catch. All 22,414 rows have an unvalidated 'period' categorical label, not a literal year. Published method: 2019 floor pitfall, 2019 subcanopy FIT, 2021 canopy FIT and four 2021 replacement plots. The paper says authors standardized individual counts per trap per day, but the released 17-column CSV does not expose a uniquely named trap-day measurement. Thus **do not compare capture abundance or causal vertical effect**.

Question for this ONE distinct, retrospective, observed-detection endpoint: does source stand TREE-TYPE identity improve prediction of *relative composition of observed feeding-guild species* in an unseen parent site within the **2019 8-ID floor stratum**, beyond the common source-guild composition? This is **out-of-parent-site predictive portability within the same original German survey**, NOT an independent country/biome confirmation, management intervention, causal host-resource effect, true zero-corrected richness or a direct test of Finland Host_tree categories.

## ONE frozen biological response and prediction

1. Once gates pass, for each plot, construct the set of **distinct source-detected species labels** using only positive-recorded species lines in the selected 8-ID floor stratum, with all original source periods pooled. Response is the **proportion of detected guild-known distinct species in each original feeding-guild category**, using the source-provided species→feedingguild record. No abundance/trap-days/residence/time standardized interpretation. If a species has conflicting nonblank feeding-guild labels anywhere in the source, scientifically STOP (no outcome-selected cleanup). Missing/empty species or guild values count as unavailable; no imputation; no species or guild names exported.
2. Read ALL original row values of the selected taxon and guild columns only after source gates. Eligibility: ≥80% source-active distinct floor species have nonmissing guild, ≥70% of detected distinct species per plot guild-known, and ≥5 guild-known detected species per plot. Require global guild number between 2 and 12, all guild classes retained; no choosing a favorable subset. If failed, STOP, no ecological estimate.
3. Plot composition `p_ig` = known-guild distinct species in category g / total known-guild distinct species in plot i; `Y_i=sqrt(p_ig)` (Hellinger representation, one vector per original plot).
4. **Out-of-site heldout**: 8 folds, exactly one parent site (its 5 stand plots) excluded at a time. Baseline = Euclidean-normalized mean of training plots' Y (all 35 plots equal weight), **one fixed common guild prediction** for all 5 heldout stand categories. Extended = Euclidean-normalized shrinkage towards common global mean of each matching original stand type's seven training-site Y vectors, with fixed shrinkage `lambda=5`: `(sum(Y_stand)+5*global_mean)/(7+5)`. Predictions never see heldout taxon response. Normalize to unit norm; loss per plot `H2=.5*sum((Y_i - prediction_i)^2)`.
5. **Primary paired estimand:** 8 parent-site-equal means of `baseline H2 − extended H2`; positive = stand identity adds transferable information. Report mean baseline and extended H2, each of eight site contrasts (ONLY anonymized sign/count/range; no site labels), overall Δ, per-site bootstrap percentile CI (4000 resamples, RNG seed 20261117), all **256 exact signflips** on the eight site contrasts, two-sided p, plus 8/0 sign counts. Do not test individual guild coefficients or stand categories.
6. **Interpretation:** even positive Δ only demonstrates guild-mixture portability *conditional on captured, identified taxa and observed source sites*, not stand resource causality, guild biomass, true taxa prevalence, climate independence, inferred insect movement, canopy thermal shelter, forest restoration effect, or cross-country predictive validation. No p-value makes it external replication of Piccini 2026 because same source data. If Δ≤0 or CI crosses zero or p≥0.05, record UNSUPPORTED for this endpoint; never rescore canopy, subcanopy, alternative λ, abundance or period subsets.
