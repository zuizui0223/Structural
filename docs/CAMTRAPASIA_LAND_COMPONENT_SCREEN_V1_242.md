# CamTrapAsia landmass geometry, not centroid-distance proxy (v1.242)

The frozen v1.241 geography-only shortlist contains 15 CamTrapAsia study centers within 25 km of a nearest original heldout island centroid, 10 candidate original islands and 7 original spatial blocks. Actual places include Pulau Bawean (2 studies), Buton (2), Tangkoko (1), Ujung Kulon National Park (5), West Sarawak, Bukit Barisan, Leuser, and Bac Hai Van; these names are not by themselves island identities.

The v1.242 geometry-only source is independent **Natural Earth 1:10m land v5.1.1** (provider https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-land/; ZIP https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/10m_physical/ne_10m_land.zip). It resolves whether a published camera survey centre and its specific nearest original heldout centroid lie on the same mapped contiguous local land component, using a predeclared half-degree local bounding rectangle. It is a **first screening coastline** and does NOT claim to be the original GADM 3.6 island polygons. GADM 3.6 country level-0 landmass component validation is a separate potential confirmatory GIS provenance test, because polygon boundaries, segmentation and tiny islands may differ.

Geographic categories must not be replaced by 'mammal-positive/mammal-negative' or interisland dispersal. A point that the 10m coastline cannot place on land is UNKNOWN, not evidence of absence; two fragments might be connected outside the fixed local clip; same land polygon is necessary but insufficient to identify a mammal's occupied population. Neither the frozen original 529 species predictions nor source camera detection records are read. GEB scientific HOLD and other closed lineages remain unchanged.
