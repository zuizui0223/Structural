## Established ecology and publication boundary

The original ForestEco2 authors already established that natural forests are more indigenous-flora-rich than exotic or production forests. That baseline is prior art, not a new Structural result. The **new attempted cross-source test** was the conditional enrichment of the 20 historical-local-loss taxa relative to other native flora, but its field numerator was insufficient.
