## Exact future original data-admission requirement and predictive hypothesis

For a **new independent forest system**, before opening a species matrix, require one author-confirmed, actual physical **site × sampling date × height layer × trap method/effort × local resource × local temperature/microclimate** ledger. Each data line should distinguish original plot from individual traps and temporal repeats, and have a non-leaking original species/OTU match. Samples without original effort are NOT true absences. Temperature/woodresource must be observed *at relevant stratum/time*, not historical 2020 Landsat tree cover or plot-general average.

Prospective question for *later* independently sampled, multi-region studies: does hostwood occurrence/identity/decay explain **out-of-region** prediction of beetle detected-site×layer composition or occupancy **conditional on environmental thermal suitability**, and does adding a *resource × thermal suitability* term improve held-out predictive log-loss/occupancy performance beyond climate, detection-effort, height, forest cover/landscape and taxonomic-composition baseline? Compare prespecified E0 climate+effort/structure, E1 + local resource, E2 + temperature×resource, freeze source field join, independent geographic exclusion and test before species labels. If compatible biological observations are insufficient, stop at methods—do not produce pseudo-precise 3D distances or source network dispersal claims.
