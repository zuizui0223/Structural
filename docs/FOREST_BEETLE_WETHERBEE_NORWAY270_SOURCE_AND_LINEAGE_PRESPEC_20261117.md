# Wetherbee 2023 Norway boreal 270-site beetles — source-only external network and original study lineage gate (2026-11-17)

## Scientific question and prior art

The original 2023 *Ecology & Evolution* Wetherbee, Birkemoe, Burner and Sverdrup-Thygeson paper [10.1002/ece3.10739](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.10739) reports **270 site samples across eight sampling projects** in southern Norway (98 near-natural and 172 managed), 339 obligate saproxylic beetle species, 70,695 individuals and source 1997–2007 sampling. The authors already analyzed relative predator abundance and beetle body morphology as forest naturalness indicators. **These are PRIOR ART**, not original Structural findings.

The authors explicitly link original data to [Zenodo record 8272489](https://zenodo.org/records/8272489). This source may overlap older Norwegian sampling projects represented in original Burner 2022 Zenodo 5950610 Norway 468-site / 1111 sample-year matrix (original collection spanning 1993–2019). Shared country and author lineage imply that **independent biological field replication MUST NOT be assumed**. A 270-site source panel is nested in EIGHT sampling-project source histories, not eight independent biomes or 270 replicate management experiments. Most authors' trapping used flying window traps over May–August; one source subproject had a different IBL-2 trap design, with some trap substrates (spruce, aspen, free-hanging) varying. Host tree identity in the trap location is NOT necessarily measured local deadwood host supply.

This audit first asks whether the full official original source archive identifies per-site original project, trapping year/method, source-sampled species and trophic/guild traits, **and an author-defined field mapping for original wood-resource values**, rather than fitting a novel effect. No original biological response is authorized now.
