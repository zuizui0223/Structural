# Forest beetle community assembly: local resource sorting versus source-independent prediction (23 Nov 2026)

## Decision first

**Two different ecological questions now have distinct empirical answers, but NOT a single universal mechanism:**
1. **Within sampled forest plots**, offered deadwood tree identity is associated with whether adult beetles emerge. The original Ecuador Falconí 2026 59 complete matched plot one-country study has exploratory within-plot five-host Cochran Q=24.3014, within-plot conditional permutation p_MC=.00005, with original host-location randomization unverified. Prior author also already found host-dependent richness and community networking — **prior art**. This is a local observation association, not source-independent forest transfer.
2. **For entirely unseen original field studies**, a coarse resource predictor does NOT necessarily generalize. Newly admitted original Traylor et al. 2026 four southeastern USA study cohorts (356 sampled wood objects, 23 source tree taxa, 141 with observed ambrosia beetle emergence) showed four-study-equal held-study log loss **E0 training-study smoothed prevalence 0.6797834 vs E1 wood volume/time/tree type/origin 0.9535479**, gain **−0.2737645**, 3/4 held studies adverse, 4-study bootstrap 95% **[−0.657235,+0.016229]**, exact four study signed p=.375. E1 had about **40.27% higher** observed out-of-study prediction error than E0. **Scientific decision: RESOURCE_PREDICTOR_TRANSFER_NOT_SUPPORTED**, NOT a significant adverse biological effect or absence of wood effect in any one forest.

Neither source establishes that host/tree substrate characteristics determine the actual plot×species community (Ecuador original 4-stage pooled taxa cannot recover original physical plot×taxon; Traylor source responds to native/exotic aggregate adult counts, not species identity). Detection/rearing zero means no observed emerged adult, not known colonization failure.

## What is actually interesting — but not yet proven

**Candidate principle:** Local substrate resource sorting can be strong while a broadly transferable substrate-only predictive rule is weak. A wood resource is potentially a necessary opportunity but it is not a sufficient state descriptor for transferable community assembly. Study-specific resource menu, available regional source pool, microclimate, activity and catch/rearing effort, wood age/decay, and source histories can change the mapping from resource to observed beetles. The available sources do NOT identify which of these mechanisms caused the transfer failure. The 23 substrate tree taxa in Traylor's four studies included only **7 shared across >=2 original studies** (source read before biological opening), so out-of-domain host substrate availability is an explicit plausible modifier, NOT an outcome-proven explanatory coefficient.

**Do not claim** new evidence of direct native/non-native competition, salvage effects or beetle fungal food web; these were either already published or untested in the new scoring. Nor call the poor coarse resource predictor a general ecological zero; it failed after leave-study-out transfer with only four studies from two SE USA areas.

## Main next experiment, not a rescoring request

A genuinely new original forest programme with:
- identified independent forest/site/region blocks, one documented physically sampled resource opportunity (log/stump/trap) including genuine recorded zero and failed collection separately;
- observed substrate tree identity and density/volume/decay/age, local microclimate or measured fungal partner state on the **same object and collection window**, source effort and method;
- matched beetle species/functional guild records with complete source sample identifiers and compatible taxon resolution;
- pre-specified and single predictor contrast, e.g. forest structure/total resource baseline versus measured object-level resource identity and decay composition, evaluated on genuinely untouched source regions/studies;
- enough independent geographical cohorts to calibrate uncertainty; four studies have minimum exact two-sided study signflip p=.125, six source site labels across non-comparable methods are not six controlled replications.
