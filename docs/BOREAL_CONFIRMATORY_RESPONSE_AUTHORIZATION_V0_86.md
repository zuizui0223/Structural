# Boreal confirmatory response authorization v0.86

v0.86 is the final access gate before the confirmatory target can be scored.

## Exact replay first

Authorization requires a complete rerun of v0.85 from the exact pilot snapshot,
geometry, habitat reference, spatial partition and v0.83 operator.

Both must match:

- the committed v0.85 receipt; and
- the complete prediction-surface bytes.

No coefficient may be refit after this authorization.

## Minimal response surface

The confirmatory router opens less than the full beetle matrix.

It may decode:

- the species header;
- Island routing fields;
- occurrence cells for the **fixed v0.84 species universe** on confirmatory
  islands.

It must not decode:

- any pilot-island occurrence cell;
- any confirmatory species outside the fixed universe.

Thus the future score opens only the targets that have already frozen
predictions.

## Authorization is not scoring

A v0.86 receipt contains no effect size and no prediction score. It simply
authorizes one later SHA-verified routing/scoring operation.

The authorization is consumed by that operation regardless of whether a
post-access domain or scoring check succeeds.
