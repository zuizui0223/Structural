# Kriegel et al. (2023): 8-country saproxylic beetle substrate-guild original macro cohort — source-only qualification (2026-11-17)

## Locked scientific reason and prior art

After Piccini 2026 original German floor-only stand-identity out-of-parent-site test was **completed and SEALED** (40 plots, 8 sites, observed 11.7% relative Hellinger² loss reduction, exact 256-signflip p=0.09375 **not supported**), seek a genuinely wider forest macro field study, rather than reselecting strata/thresholds.

Independently public [Kriegel et al. 2023 *Ecology Letters* DOI 10.1111/ele.14227](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.14227), source [Dryad 10.5061/dryad.brv15dvch](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.brv15dvch), reports **21 source datasets from eight European countries**, 332,557 saproxylic beetle individuals, 901 beetle species reared from **49 tree species**. Published manuscript reports xylophagous versus mycetophagous/zoophagous differences in substrate versus ambient-energy filtering; **that is prior art, not a new Structural finding**. Methods require each beetle's emergence assignable to a wood object, one major improvement over flight-trap occupancy; however the 21 source datasets can have differing protocols and reused physical samples. The European source is not necessarily independent of any previous German/BELongDead data.

The prospective *different* test is whether wood-resource host identity and climatic context improve **out-of-original-study-group** prediction of feeding-guild assemblage composition, rather than in-sample energy effects; 21 dataset tags are not 21 independent biomes, and leave-country-out would only have eight possible geographic blocks. Future test must require author study/wood-object/year/host and guild join with full sampling/detection effort, site and source lineage before response access. Do not prespec a favorable subgroup outcome yet.
