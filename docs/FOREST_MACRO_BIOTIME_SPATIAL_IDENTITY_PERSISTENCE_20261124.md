## New estimand (method development; not novel claim before systematic prior-art audit)
For matched physical sites in one study, habitat and consecutive year pair, represent each observed community by a fixed study-local Hellinger incidence vector (same taxon axis). Center site vectors separately in each year: U_i = X_i - mean(X), V_i = Y_i - mean(Y). Report:
- spatial variances S0 = mean ||U||², S1 = mean ||V||²;
- C = mean dot(U,V), and alignment A = C/sqrt(S0*S1) when both spatial variances are nonzero;
- raw temporal change T = mean ||X-Y||².
A measures persistence of cross-site composition contrasts, not causal memory. Algebraically C equals mean pairwise lagged cross-site distance minus same-site lag distance under balanced pair weighting with the appropriate scaling. Shared additive vector shifts disappear from centered contrast; composition normalization can alter this property. In a completely homogenized year A is undefined, not zero; report variance collapse separately, do not discard it and call remaining cases representative. Actual sampling coverage and site count must be comparable; same cohort and effort; no site-ID inference or zero padding. Only observed matched communities, not detection-adjusted occupancy. Permutations of site labels require spatial/management exchangeability; no calibrated p-values before that design and parent-study units are audited. Within-study disturbance comparison to be frozen after source-only eligibility, no pooled estimates across non-comparable treatment meanings.

## Executed synthetic tests

- identical 3 sites: A=1, T=0.
- common taxon replaced everywhere, local contrasts unchanged: A=1, T=1.
- local species identities permuted cyclically, same annual pooled species composition: A=-0.5, T=1.
- sites homogenize: S1=0, C=0, A undefined, T=2/3.
This is an algebraic/ecological diagnostic, not a new ecological effect. Stable unmeasured environment can preserve spatial identity without direct biotic interaction.
