# Forest macroecology hypothesis shortlist: the resource-recovery paradox (2026-11-24)

## Primary ecological hypothesis — history-dependent *realized* forest beetle community

**A forest's current deadwood inventory does not uniquely determine its saproxylic beetle community.** The original regional source pool, past uninterrupted availability of particular deadwood resources, and earlier colonizing insects/fungi can modify which specialist functional channels become realized; these may remain depleted **even if present standing/lying wood carbon or volume recovers**. This is a hypothesis, not a confirmed general law.

It is NOT novel to say host tree matters, that dispersal/colonization comes before host selection, that deadwood quantity and continuity matter, or that beetles can change later fungal succession: original literature has already studied all of these mechanisms. Proposed novelty is a **joint quantitative, held-region, resource-state-controlled test of the conditions causing a residual specialist/functional deficit**, with an external source-pool census and time-aligned wood history.

## Four falsifiable alternatives, ordered by ecological priority

### H1. Resource recovery produces a *biotic assembly debt* (PRIMARY)
Compare original forest sites matched on PRESENT measured standing and lying wood volumes, hostwood tree-type/decay-stage availability, climate, area/structure, survey method and effort, but differing in objectively dated historical RESOURCE CONTINUITY and INDEPENDENTLY sampled source-guild availability (not the heldout target beetles).
- Prediction: recently restored high-deadwood sites with a prior gap have lower incidence of narrow-host/late-decay/specialist beetle guilds and lower specialist functional diversity than continuously supplied sites, despite similar present total wood and even similar total observed beetle richness.
- Interaction: historical deadwood interruption should have strongest continuing effects where independently known appropriate colonist source populations are distant/scarce; better resource amounts alone do not compensate.
- Falsifier: after time/method/wood composition and independent source availability, historical interruption has no repeatable held-region incremental value; or the pattern disappears under detection correction.
- Conservation implication if supported: conserving resource continuity and source populations may be more effective than only meeting a current volume target.

### H2. Rare resource *channels* not total wood limit functional assembly
- Prediction: with equal **total deadwood volume and tree-species richness**, the absence of a particular underrepresented wood state (e.g. large diameter late-decay standing tree vs small fallen early-decay log) eliminates characteristic specialist guilds, increasing trait-selective absence, while broad host-generalists may compensate raw species richness.
- Differential prediction from basic amount hypothesis: multiple forests can have the SAME total wood/richness yet distinct species identity and trait-channel occupancy; matched resource types recover guilds only where the independent source pool includes candidate colonists.
- Falsifier: wood total/geometry predicts heldout trait composition as well as source-measured resource state and source pool; no type-specific vacancy after effort calibration.
- Beware: standing snag benefit and tree-genus/decay sorting are PRIOR ART; only a joint compensatory-diversity / resource absence contrast is potentially different.

### H3. Same measured wood can follow multiple paths (biotic priority/history feedback)
- Prediction: in matched wood pieces with same age, species, diameter, microclimate and bark condition, independently observed early mycetophagous/wood-boring arrivals and early fungal taxa predict LATER colonist guild identity beyond current physical decay and wood quantity.
- Interaction: early source-pool isolation changes the chance of particular pioneer insects/fungi, creating persistent alternative fungal/late-beetle trajectories.
- Falsifier: independent early arrivals do not add predictive value over matched decay/microclimate, or results depend exclusively on source-method detection.
- Prior art: Weslien et al. 2011 (15yr spruce); Ottosson et al. 2015 (aspen early insects to later fungi); Neff et al. 2022 hierarchical trait filtering; Seibold/Lettenmaier repeated hostwood succession. No causal feedback without interventions/exclusion/pioneer manipulations.

### H4. More deadwood can restore alpha richness without restoring guild identity (compensation paradox)
- Prediction: after a standardized source-method restoration treatment, higher total deadwood causes increased/maintained species counts through abundant, mobile broad host guilds while source-limited, low-dispersal and late-decay specialists are still absent; abundance, alpha, beta replacement and functional diversity diverge.
- Stronger test: matched future restored and continuously supplied forests; pre/post community identity replacement with independent effort and historic reference community. Numeric richness recovery alone cannot be used to claim restored ecological functioning.
- Falsifier: specialist guild identity, functional composition and richness all recover proportionately after controlling detection, source pool and resource state.
- Functional diversity is trait-dependent: preserve original feeding/wood-host/dispersal traits with independent coverage, never invent "biomass" from trap counts or assume body length equals biomass.

## Sharp test design for an actual new macro cohort
Use multiple independent study programmes with original **forest×year×wood-state inventory** (host tree identity + standing/lying, diameter + decay, wood supply history), trap effort/zero deployments, physical forest site hierarchy and species-level beetle responses. An independent earlier regional beetle census with consistent taxonomy/method estimates source-pool *observed presence*; unknown is NOT absent. Do not derive donor records from heldout target organisms. Stratify across restoration/continuity and available source pools without turning geographic 100 km survey proximity into a dispersal distance.

**Primary contrast**: held-region fixed ecological response specialist-guild vacancy (observed detections or corrected occupancy with measured effort) conditional on current resource quality, versus plus *historical resource continuity × independently observed guild source availability*. Additional response functional composition/richness is supporting and must not be p-selected. Old data endpoints cannot be rerun or stacked as independent regions.

An actual experimental validation would deploy equal hostwood/decay-state units in source-rich/source-poor matched forests and track visits separately from beetle emergence, fungal communities and wood condition over multiple years; avoid post hoc planted beetle labels as source pools.
