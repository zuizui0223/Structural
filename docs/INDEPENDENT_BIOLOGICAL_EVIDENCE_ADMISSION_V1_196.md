# Structural v1.196 — independent biological evidence admission audit

## Why this audit exists

A geometry-based graph can improve held-out predictions of an IUCN-range-derived mammal matrix without identifying the natural dispersal network. **The missing observation is independent biological occupancy or movement**, not another same-data network-index contrast.

This document is a source/transport/denominator audit only. It changes none of the frozen 529-species, v1.181 source-turnover or BALA result values.

## 1. What the independent attempts actually established

**ALA Australian island mammal records.** The response-independent GIS crosswalk resolved 167 held-out islands. A taxon-column audit matched 70 original focal taxa and excluded five, leaving 65 provisional. The one-shot `v1.194` source archive did expose some eligible species×island×year positives and passed the minimum support gate in its control flow, but stopped before independent prediction scoring on a proven two-header-versus-three-header binary parser mismatch. It is a **technical post-pair-access terminal STOP**, not positive or negative ecological evidence. Replaying the consumed external labels as new confirmation is not authorized.

**Hébert et al. 2021 checklists** (DOI 10.5061/dryad.rfj6q579r). The documentation describes 204 islands, nine island groups and independently compiled literature/atlas lists with nonuniform historical and IUCN overlaps. The first header-only GitHub execution, [v1.188 run 37727347706](https://github.com/zuizui0223/Structural/actions/runs/37727347706), stopped at the **first file**, `Adr.csv`, because both originally frozen public Dryad download endpoints failed. No external files were successfully projected; consequently no species header, island name or 0/1 occurrence body has been audited, and no original held-out mammal labels were reopened. The v1.189 provenance-classification contract is already frozen from published documentation; it is *not* a successful island/taxon crosswalk.

The six provisionally literature-primary archipelagos are Alexander, Adriatic, Sundaland, Maluku, Melanesia and the Philippines. California Gulf cites IUCN and is excluded from an independent primary; the two Mediterranean groups are held for mixed historical/zooarchaeological sources. All six provisional pools still require actual source and taxonomy checks once metadata transport is resolvable.

The next step is **not** to silently relax the v1.188 taxon, island or archipelago gates, or read matrix 0/1 directly. It is either to verify a transport route for the *same exact source files* before any semantic body access or to leave the route on transport HOLD. An alternate dataset is not a substitute for a fixed-route transport retry.

## 2. A direct movement dataset is not automatically a movement-process test

The open metadata for Li et al. 2026 on Danish Arctic terns (DOI 10.5061/dryad.x69p8czxh) contain 890 natal and 200 breeding **mover events** with departure/arrival coordinates. These are much closer to observed movement than IUCN range polygons.

But the authors say the raw ringing records and non-movers are not shared, nor is a complete pre-event candidate breeding-colony choice set. If the candidate destinations are constructed from the observed arrivals, the *choice set itself is selected by the outcome*. Thus the tables can describe **where recorded movers went**, but cannot by themselves measure colonization probability, demographic rescue, or displacement risk. They also cannot validate the mammal model without an independent cross-taxon model and destination denominator.

Do not promote attractive but endpoint-mismatched data merely because they contain coordinates.

## 3. Three independent evidence gates

**Independent occupancy:** unique species and island identities fixed without viewing external positives/zeros, independently verified site-native and time-appropriate observations, then frozen original R3/C predictions scored without refit. Zero labels from opportunistic presence-only sources are not surveyed absence.

**Realized dispersal:** movement records plus a non-outcome-selected set of potential origins/destinations, explicit effort/ascertainment or a clearly *conditional-on-observed-moving* estimand, comparison against strong Euclidean distance and geography-based nulls.

**Temporal ecological consequence:** t0 source state, a recorded t0→t1 source change, t1→t2 survival/colonization outcome with detection denominator. ALA location positives and mover-only tern events cannot replace this.

## Current scientific conclusion

**Still supported:** within the curated Barreto/IUCN map, a geography-derived kNN operator improves prediction for a preregistered 529-species mapped-positive layer; its original graph outperformed its frozen coarse rewired nulls.

**Still unsupported or unresolved:** individual source-territory irreplaceability (v1.181 adverse), temporal BALA source-loss leverage (non-support), independent contemporary mammal presence validation (no score), biological dispersal pathways, causal colonization/rescue and management priority.

GEB remains on scientific HOLD for those stronger ecological claims. No additional same-mammal threshold mining, ALA post-exposure rescue, BALA retest, SW Finland same-archive rescue or eBird analysis.
