# Lettenmaier 12-year design identifiability (2026-11-24)

## Verified from author methods and executed design calculation
Source: JAE DOI 10.1111/1365-2656.70183, §2.1–2.3. Beech/fir exposure age 1 in 2013; spruce age 0 in 2013. Sampling absent 2020, yearly emergence traps relocated. Original authors already tested host-specific age trends, community turnover, functional diversity and temporal niches. No new ecological discovery from reproducing those results.
For all three hosts and the 11 nominal observed calendar years, age = calendar year - 2013 + 1 - I(spruce). Full nominal 33-row, 5-column design (intercept, two host indicators, numeric calendar year, exposure age) has rank 4, as independently computed in code. Among 27 nominal host×adjacent-year target cells, 24 have earlier same-host transition cells; all 24 target ages exceed the earlier same-host age range. Real source missingness can reduce support. Age and calendar effects cannot be independently identified in this cohort. Strict temporal forecasting is not a pure estimate of history, and necessarily includes age extrapolation within host.

A can isolate within-cohort predictive information, not temporal transfer; B additionally measures extrapolation. Do not treat either as causal.
